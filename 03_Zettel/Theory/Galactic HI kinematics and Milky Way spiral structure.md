neutral hydrogen $21\text{ cm}$ emission is the primary kinematic tracer of the Milky Way's disk, immune to interstellar dust extinction.

## the tangent point method

inside the Solar circle ($R < R_0$, first and fourth Galactic quadrants):

$$v_r(l, R) = \left(\frac{\Theta(R)}{R} - \frac{\Theta_0}{R_0}\right) R_0 \sin l$$

along any line of sight with longitude $0^\circ < l < 90^\circ$, the maximum line-of-sight radial velocity (the **terminal velocity** $v_{\text{term}}$) occurs at the tangent point where the line of sight is perpendicular to the circular orbit ($R_{\text{min}} = R_0 \sin l$):

$$\Theta(R_{\text{min}}) = v_{\text{term}}(l) + \Theta_0 \sin l$$

measuring $v_{\text{term}}(l)$ yields the circular velocity $\Theta(R)$ without distance ambiguity.

## milestone #1 revision: grand-design vs flocculent inner disk

- **classical model (Oort, Westerhout, Schmidt 1957)**: mapped terminal velocities into a continuous 4-arm grand-design spiral pattern (Norma, Scutum-Centaurus, Sagittarius-Carina, Perseus).
- **modern revision (Balser & Burton 2025/2026 AJ)**: re-analyzed high-resolution HI4PI all-sky data. they demonstrated that the high-velocity wings show no shallow shoulders or broad dips corresponding to grand-design inter-arm gaps. the inner Milky Way gas disk is structurally **flocculent**, dominated by short, overlapping spiral fragments rather than grand-design symmetry.

## warp and flaring in the outer disk

1. **Galactic Warp**: beyond $R \sim 12\text{ kpc}$, the H I midplane curves up to $z \sim +1.5\text{ kpc}$ in the north ($l \sim 90^\circ$) and down to $z \sim -1.0\text{ kpc}$ in the south ($l \sim 270^\circ$).
2. **Disk Flaring**: the vertical scale height $h_z$ of the H I layer increases from $\sim 150\text{ pc}$ at $R_0$ to $> 1\text{ kpc}$ at $R \sim 25\text{ kpc}$, driven by the weakening vertical gravitational restoring force of the stellar disk.
