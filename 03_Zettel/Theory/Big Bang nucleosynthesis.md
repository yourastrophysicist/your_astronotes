**Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (BBN)** is the production of light elements ($^4$He, D, $^3$He, $^7$Li) in the first few minutes after the Big Bang. the only place outside stellar interiors where light elements are forged. one of the three pillars of the Hot Big Bang. (companion: [BBN_overview](./BBN_overview.html) + family of BBN notes for details.)

## the timeline

| event | $T$ | $t$ |
|---|---|---|
| neutrino decoupling | $1$ MeV | $\sim 1$ s |
| $e^\pm$ annihilation | $0.5$ MeV | $\sim 30$ s |
| **deuterium production** | $0.07$ MeV | $\sim 3$ min |
| BBN ends | $0.03$ MeV | $\sim 20$ min |

BBN happens in a **very narrow window** around $T \sim 0.1$ MeV, when the temperature is just right for nuclear reactions to fuse but not high enough for photodissociation to destroy them.

## the bottleneck

before $T \sim 0.07$ MeV, **deuterium is destroyed** by Wien-tail photons faster than it forms. but $T$ is high enough for nuclear reactions.

once $T$ drops to $\sim 0.07$ MeV, deuterium **survives** + becomes the seed for further fusion: D + p → $^3$He, D + D → $^3$He / $^3$T, etc., eventually producing $^4$He.

since fusion to $^4$He is much faster than escape via $^7$Li (the "lithium gap"), almost all neutrons end up in $^4$He.

## the predicted abundances

light element fractions (mass fractions $Y_X$):
- **$^4$He**: $Y_{\rm He} \approx 0.247$. **dominantly determined by $\eta = n_b/n_\gamma$**.
- **D/H**: $\sim 2.5 \times 10^{-5}$. very sensitive to $\eta$.
- **$^3$He/H**: $\sim 1 \times 10^{-5}$.
- **$^7$Li/H**: $\sim 5 \times 10^{-10}$.

## the baryon-to-photon ratio $\eta$

$\eta$ is the **only free parameter** in standard BBN (assuming standard model + 3 neutrino species). measuring light-element abundances pins down $\eta$.

modern values from BBN abundances:
$$\eta_{\rm BBN} = 6.1 \times 10^{-10}$$

equivalent to:
$$\Omega_b h^2 = 0.0224$$

## the agreement with CMB

independent measurement: CMB acoustic peak structure gives $\Omega_b h^2$:
$$\Omega_b h^2 = 0.0224 \pm 0.0001\quad(\text{Planck})$$

**identical** to BBN value to high precision. **the great triumph** of $\Lambda$CDM: two independent probes of the universe at $z \sim 10^9$ + $z \sim 1100$ giving the same baryon density.

## the lithium problem

predicted $^7$Li/H: $\sim 5 \times 10^{-10}$. observed in metal-poor halo stars: $\sim 1.5 \times 10^{-10}$, **factor of 3 lower** than predicted.

possible solutions:
- **stellar diffusion**: lithium destroyed at stellar surfaces over Gyr.
- **cosmic-ray spallation**: extra production not accounted for.
- **new physics** beyond standard BBN: e.g. decaying particles, modified gravity.

active area of research. most likely stellar physics, but uncertain.

## the constraint on neutrino species

extra neutrino species would speed up the early-universe expansion, decrease the freeze-out time of n/p ratio, increase $^4$He. BBN constraints:
$$N_{\rm eff} = 2.99 \pm 0.34$$

consistent with the standard 3 light neutrinos. rules out extra relativistic species at the $\sim 1$ unit level.
