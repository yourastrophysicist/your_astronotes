carbonaceous compounds in the interstellar medium manifest through distinct spectroscopic signatures spanning optical absorption to mid-infrared emission.

## diffuse interstellar bands (dibs)

first detected by Mary Lea Heger (1919), DIBs are over 500 ubiquitous optical absorption features ($\lambda 4430, 5780, 5797, 6614\text{ \AA}$) produced by intervening interstellar material.

properties:
- their Doppler shifts track interstellar gas clouds rather than background stellar photospheres.
- equivalent widths correlate with dust reddening $E(B-V)$, but with significant line-of-sight scatter.
- Carraro slide 26 notes the harmonic relation: $\lambda 4430 \approx 2 \times 2175\text{ \AA}$ and $\lambda 6614 \approx 3 \times 2175\text{ \AA}$.
- **confirmed carrier**: in 2015, laboratory spectroscopy confirmed the buckminsterfullerene cation ($C_{60}^+$) as the carrier of two near-infrared DIBs at $9577\text{ \AA}$ and $9632\text{ \AA}$.

## unidentified infrared bands (uibs) and pahs

observed in emission in the mid-infrared ($3 - 20\text{ }\mu\text{m}$):
- prominent peaks at $3.3, 6.2, 7.7, 8.6, 11.3\text{ }\mu\text{m}$.
- **carriers**: Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs), planar molecules of fused benzene rings.
- **quantum heating**: because PAHs contain only tens to hundreds of atoms, absorption of a single UV photon ($h\nu \sim 10\text{ eV}$) spikes their internal temperature to $T \sim 1000\text{ K}$. cooling proceeds via vibrational transitions:
  - $3.3\text{ }\mu\text{m}$: aromatic C-H stretch
  - $6.2\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ and $7.7\text{ }\mu\text{m}$: aromatic C-C ring stretch
  - $8.6\text{ }\mu\text{m}$: C-H in-plane bend
  - $11.3\text{ }\mu\text{m}$: C-H out-of-plane bend

PAH emission serves as an extinction-free tracer of star formation in galaxies.
