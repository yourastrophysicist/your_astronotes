# Hot versus cold dark matter in the early universe

> *free-streaming scales, structure formation regimes, and the decisive observational victory of cold over hot dark matter.*

---

## core physical intuition

Dark matter candidates are classified by their velocity dispersion at the epoch of structure formation:
* **Hot Dark Matter (HDM)**: Relativistic at freeze-out ($T_{\rm fo} \gg m_\chi$).
* **Cold Dark Matter (CDM)**: Non-relativistic at freeze-out ($T_{\rm fo} \ll m_\chi$).

Because HDM particles move near the speed of light, they stream freely out of primordial gravitational wells, completely erasing density fluctuations on scales smaller than the free-streaming horizon. This forces a **top-down** history where superclusters form first and fragment into galaxies. Because observations show that galaxies and globular clusters formed first, HDM cannot be the dominant dark matter.

---

## key derivation & equations

### 1. Hot Dark Matter (e.g. Standard Model Neutrinos)
Decouple at $T \sim 1\text{ MeV} \gg m_\nu$.
Relic density:
$$n_\nu(t_0) = \frac{3}{11} n_\gamma(t_0) \approx 112\text{ cm}^{-3} \text{ per flavor}$$
$$\Omega_\nu h^2 = \frac{\sum m_\nu}{93.14\text{ eV}}$$
The comoving **free-streaming scale** is:
$$\lambda_{\rm fs} = \int_0^{t_{\rm nr}} \frac{c\, dt'}{a(t')} \approx 40\text{ Mpc} \left(\frac{30\text{ eV}}{m_\nu}\right)$$
Fluctuations with $k > k_{\rm fs} = 2\pi/\lambda_{\rm fs}$ are completely washed out by collisionless damping.

### 2. Cold Dark Matter (e.g. WIMPs, Axions)
Decouple when non-relativistic ($x_f = m/T_f \approx 25$).
The free-streaming scale is negligible ($\lambda_{\rm fs} \ll 1\text{ pc}$).
Density perturbations survive on all astrophysically relevant scales, giving rise to **bottom-up hierarchical clustering**: small halos collapse first, then merge into galaxies, groups, and clusters.

---

## astrophysical context

* Galaxy power spectrum $P(k)$ measured by SDSS, 2dFGRS, and BOSS, which matches $\Lambda$CDM perfectly and rules out pure HDM models.
* Cosmological upper limit on the sum of neutrino masses: $\sum m_\nu < 0.12\text{ eV}$ (Planck + BAO).
