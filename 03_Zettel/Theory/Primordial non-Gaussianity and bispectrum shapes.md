# Primordial non-Gaussianity and bispectrum shapes

> *how the triangular geometry of three-point correlators reveals the microphysics and particle interactions of the primordial universe.*

---

## core physical intuition

The two-point power spectrum tells us about the energy scale and speed of inflation, but cannot distinguish between single-field models and complex multi-field or higher-derivative theories. The three-point function (the bispectrum) breaks this degeneracy: its dependence on the shape of the momentum triangle $\vec{k}_1 + \vec{k}_2 + \vec{k}_3 = 0$ acts as a direct spectrogram of the fields and interactions operating during inflation.

---

## key derivation & equations

The bispectrum $B_\zeta$ is defined by:
$$\langle \zeta_{\vec{k}_1}\zeta_{\vec{k}_2}\zeta_{\vec{k}_3} \rangle = (2\pi)^3 \delta^{(3)}(\vec{k}_1 + \vec{k}_2 + \vec{k}_3) B_\zeta(k_1, k_2, k_3)$$

### The three primary shapes
1. **Local shape** ($k_1 \ll k_2 \approx k_3$, squeezed limit):
   $$B_\zeta^{\rm local}(k_1, k_2, k_3) = \frac{6}{5} f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local} \left[ P_\zeta(k_1)P_\zeta(k_2) + P_\zeta(k_1)P_\zeta(k_3) + P_\zeta(k_2)P_\zeta(k_3) \right]$$
   Generated by super-horizon non-linearities in multi-field models (curvaton, modulated reheating).
2. **Equilateral shape** ($k_1 \approx k_2 \approx k_3$):
   Vanishes in the squeezed limit; peaks when all modes cross the horizon simultaneously.
   Generated by derivative self-interactions in models with non-canonical kinetic terms ($c_s \ll 1$):
   $$f_{\rm NL}^{\rm equil} \approx -\frac{1}{c_s^2}$$
3. **Orthogonal / Folded shape** ($k_1 + k_2 \approx k_3$):
   Generated by non-Bunch-Davies initial states or Galileon-type derivative couplings.

### Planck 2018 observational bounds
* $f_{\rm NL}^{\rm local} = -0.9 \pm 5.1$
* $f_{\rm NL}^{\rm equil} = -26 \pm 47$
* $f_{\rm NL}^{\rm ortho} = -38 \pm 24$

---

## astrophysical context

* CMB bispectrum measured from Planck temperature and polarization multipoles.
* Scale-dependent halo bias in large-scale galaxy surveys (Euclid, DESI, Vera Rubin Observatory).
