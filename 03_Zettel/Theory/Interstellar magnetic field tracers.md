title: "Interstellar magnetic field tracers"
---
interstellar magnetic fields ($B \sim \text{few } \mu\text{G}$) are mapped across phases using four distinct techniques:

| method | physical mechanism | field component probed | medium traced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **starlight polarization** | selective extinction by aligned spinning dust grains (Davis-Greenstein) | $\mathbf{B}_\perp$ (plane of sky) | diffuse dusty ISM |
| **polarized dust emission** | thermal FIR/sub-mm emission along the long grain axis | $\mathbf{B}_\perp$ (perpendicular to polarization) | dense clouds, cold dust |
| **Zeeman effect** | line splitting $\Delta \nu_Z = \frac{g_L e B_\parallel}{4\pi m_e c}$ ($1.4\text{ Hz}/\mu\text{G}$ for 21 cm) | $B_\parallel$ (line of sight) | neutral clouds (HI), masers (OH) |
| **Faraday rotation** | plasma birefringence $\Delta \theta = \text{RM} \lambda^2$ | $B_\parallel$ (line of sight, $\propto \int n_e B_\parallel ds$) | WIM, pulsars, radio galaxies |
| **synchrotron radiation** | relativistic cosmic ray electrons in magnetic fields | $\mathbf{B}_\perp$ and total field | diffuse disk, halos, SNRs |
