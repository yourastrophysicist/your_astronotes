# standard lcdm parameters

up: [Observational_Cosmology_MOC](../../04_Atlas/Observational_Cosmology_MOC.html) · [Fundamentals_Astrophysics_Cosmology_MOC](../../04_Atlas/Fundamentals_Astrophysics_Cosmology_MOC.html)

## planck 2018 baseline parameters

The standard cosmological model ($\Lambda$CDM) is specified by six primary parameters determined with sub-percent precision by the Planck 2018 cosmic microwave background temperature, polarization, and lensing data:

| parameter | symbol | planck 2018 best-fit | physical meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| physical baryon density | $\Omega_b h^2$ | $0.02237 \pm 0.00015$ | cosmic baryon fraction from acoustic peak heights |
| physical cold dark matter density | $\Omega_c h^2$ | $0.1200 \pm 0.0012$ | non-baryonic matter driving gravitational potential wells |
| acoustic scale angle | $100\,\theta_{\rm MC}$ | $1.04092 \pm 0.00031$ | angular size of the sound horizon at recombination $\theta_* = r_s / d_A$ |
| reionization optical depth | $\tau$ | $0.0544 \pm 0.0073$ | Thomson scattering optical depth from reionized IGM ($z_{\rm reion} \approx 7.7$) |
| scalar spectral index | $n_s$ | $0.9649 \pm 0.0042$ | primordial tilt of scalar perturbation spectrum ($n_s < 1$ confirms slow-roll inflation) |
| primordial amplitude | $\ln(10^{10} A_s)$ | $3.044 \pm 0.014$ | amplitude of primordial curvature perturbations at pivot scale $k_0 = 0.05\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$ |

## derived cosmological parameters

- **Hubble constant**: $H_0 = 67.36 \pm 0.54$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$ ($h = 0.6736$).
- **Total matter density**: $\Omega_m = \frac{\Omega_b h^2 + \Omega_c h^2}{h^2} = 0.3153 \pm 0.0073$.
- **Dark energy density**: $\Omega_\Lambda = 1 - \Omega_m = 0.6847 \pm 0.0073$.
- **Spatial curvature**: $\Omega_k = 0.0007 \pm 0.0019$ (strictly flat, $k=0$).
- **Matter fluctuation amplitude**: $\sigma_8 = 0.8111 \pm 0.0060$ (RMS density fluctuations in $8 h^{-1}$ Mpc spheres).
- **Sound horizon at drag epoch**: $r_d = 147.09 \pm 0.26$ Mpc (standard ruler for BAO).
- **Age of the universe**: $t_0 = 13.787 \pm 0.020$ Gyr.

## the hubble tension

Direct local measurements of the distance ladder using Cepheids and Type Ia supernovae (SH0ES collaboration, Riess et al. 2022) find:
$$H_0 = 73.04 \pm 1.04 \text{ km s}^{-1} \text{ Mpc}^{-1}$$
This discrepancy with Planck CMB results exceeds $5\sigma$ significance, representing a potential breakdown of base $\Lambda$CDM (e.g., early dark energy, decaying dark matter, or modified neutrino physics).
