the interstellar medium is not a single homogeneous gas, but a multi-phase system spanning five orders of magnitude in temperature ($10 - 10^7\text{ K}$) and eight orders of magnitude in density ($10^{-4} - 10^6\text{ cm}^{-3}$).

## the six empirical phases

coexisting in the Milky Way disk (McKee & Ostriker 1977 framework):

| phase | $T$ (K) | $n$ ($\text{cm}^{-3}$) | $f_V$ | $h$ (pc) | primary state | tracers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **molecular clouds** | $10 - 20$ | $10^2 - 10^6$ | $< 1\%$ | $\sim 80$ | $H_2$ | CO rotational lines (115 GHz), sub-mm dust emission |
| **cold neutral medium (CNM)** | $50 - 100$ | $20 - 50$ | $1 - 5\%$ | $100 - 300$ | H I | 21 cm absorption against continuum |
| **warm neutral medium (WNM)** | $6000 - 10000$ | $0.2 - 0.5$ | $10 - 20\%$ | $300 - 400$ | H I | 21 cm emission |
| **warm ionized medium (WIM)** | $\approx 8000$ | $0.2 - 0.5$ | $20 - 50\%$ | $\sim 1000$ | $H^+$ | diffuse H$\alpha$ emission, pulsar dispersion ($DM$) |
| **H II regions** | $\approx 8000$ | $10^2 - 10^4$ | $< 1\%$ | $\sim 70$ | $H^+$ | optical recombination lines, radio recombination, [O III] |
| **hot ionized medium (HIM)** | $10^6 - 10^7$ | $10^{-4} - 10^{-2}$ | $30 - 70\%$ | $1000 - 3000$ | highly ionized | soft X-rays, FUV resonance absorption (O VI, C IV) |

## pressure equilibrium

to zeroth order, these phases share a roughly comparable thermal pressure:

$$P_{\text{th}}/k = n T \sim 10^3 - 10^4\text{ K cm}^{-3}$$

the thermal balance between photoelectric heating from small dust grains/PAHs and radiative cooling from fine-structure lines ([C II] $158\text{ }\mu\text{m}$, [O I] $63\text{ }\mu\text{m}$) produces an S-shaped equilibrium curve in the $P - n$ plane (the Field, Goldsmith, & Habing 1969 two-phase model). at interstellar thermal pressures $P/k \sim 3000\text{ K cm}^{-3}$, two stable thermal solutions coexist: the cold dense CNM and the warm diffuse WNM.

total interstellar pressure includes essential non-thermal components:

$$P_{\text{tot}} = P_{\text{th}} + P_{\text{turb}} + P_B + P_{\text{CR}} \sim 2 - 3 \times 10^4\text{ K cm}^{-3}$$

where magnetic pressure $P_B = B^2/8\pi$, turbulent ram pressure $P_{\text{turb}} = \rho \sigma_v^2$, and cosmic ray pressure $P_{\text{CR}}$ contribute roughly equal shares ($\sim 1\text{ eV cm}^{-3}$) in the Solar circle.
