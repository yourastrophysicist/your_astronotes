space-based transit photometry circumvents atmospheric scintillation, diurnal day-night cycles, and weather interruptions, enabling continuous micro-magnitude precision.

## space transit survey evolution

| mission | agency | aperture / architecture | primary target / scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CoRoT** (2006–2013) | CNES/ESA | 27 cm telescope | first space rocky exoplanet (CoRoT-7b) |
| **Kepler** (2009–2018) | NASA | 95 cm Schmidt, 115 deg$^2$ FOV | exoplanet statistics, occurrence rate $\eta_\oplus$, discovered $> 2700$ planets |
| **K2** (2014–2018) | NASA | 2-reaction-wheel ecliptic survey | diverse stellar populations, young clusters, multi-planet systems (K2-19) |
| **TESS** (2018–present) | NASA | 4 wide-angle lenses ($24^\circ \times 96^\circ$) | all-sky survey of bright, nearby stars for RV and atmospheric follow-up |

## the cheops mission (esa s-class, 2019-present)

CHEOPS (Characterising ExOPlanet Satellite) is a targeted follow-up mission:
- **instrument**: 30 cm Ritchey-Chrétien telescope in low Earth Sun-synchronous orbit.
- **defocused PSF**: deliberate optical defocusing spreads starlight across $\sim 300$ pixels, averaging pixel response non-uniformity and achieving ultra-high photometric stability ($< 20\text{ ppm}$ over 6 hours).
- **objective**: measuring precise radii of known transiting planets to constrain bulk densities and interior structure.

## the plato mission (esa m3 class, launch 2026)

PLATO (PLAnetary Transits and Oscillations of stars) aims to detect and characterize terrestrial habitable-zone planets around solar-type stars:
- **camera array**: 26 cameras (24 "normal" cameras with 25 s cadence, 2 "fast" cameras with 2.5 s cadence) with combined entrance pupil diameter $> 1\text{ m}$ and field of view $> 2100\text{ deg}^2$.
- **focal plane**: $> 2.1$ Gigapixels across 104 CCDs.
- **asteroseismology synergy**: measuring stellar oscillations simultaneously with transits to derive stellar masses, radii, and ages to within $10\%$, providing absolute ages for terrestrial worlds.
