# TESS SAP vs PDCSAP Flux and Cotrending Basis Vectors

TESS Light Curve Files provide two distinct light curve data products:

## 1. Simple Aperture Photometry (`SAP_FLUX`)
- Calculated by summing pixel intensities within the aperture mask and subtracting estimated local background.
- Preserves raw physical stellar variability.
- Corrupted by instrument systematics: pointing jitter, thermal expansion cycles, and Earth/Moon scattered light.

## 2. Pre-search Data Conditioning SAP (`PDCSAP_FLUX`)
- Generated by the SPOC pipeline to remove instrumental systematics while preserving planetary transits.
- **Cotrending Basis Vectors (CBVs)**: orthogonal trend vectors extracted via Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) across hundreds of quiescent reference stars on the same CCD:
$$F_{\text{PDCSAP}}(t) = F_{\text{SAP}}(t) - \sum_{k=1}^K a_k \, \text{CBV}_k(t)$$
- Penalized maximum likelihood fits the weights $a_k$ without absorbing true astrophysical features.

## Quality Bitmask Exclusions
Before transit modeling, cadences with non-zero quality bits indicating momentum dumps (`Bit 6`), attitude tweaks (`Bit 1`), or scattered light (`Bit 5`) must be removed:
$$\text{clean\_mask} = (\text{QUALITY} \,\&\, \text{critical\_bits}) == 0$$
