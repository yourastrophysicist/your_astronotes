the **Astrophysics of Galaxies** course at U Padova, AY 2025/2026, second semester (24 Feb to 20 May). taught Cassata + Morelli + others. exam: **Monday 6 July 2026**.

## what this course is

unlike Fundamentals (broad survey) or OC (cosmological observables), this course goes deep on **the internal physics of galaxies**: how their photometry, spectra, kinematics, dark matter, and central black holes are measured + modelled.

the central question:
> *what is a galaxy, physically, and what does its anatomy tell us about how it formed?*

## the chain of analysis

every galaxy observation walks through this chain:
$$\text{photometry} \to \text{morphology} \to \text{spectra} \to \text{kinematics} \to \text{stellar populations} \to \text{dark matter halo} \to \text{evolutionary history}$$

each block of the course handles one piece. the exam expects fluency at every link.

## the lecture rhythm

24 weekly lectures spanning:
- **early weeks**: photometric surveys, LF, classification (Cassata).
- **mid weeks**: spectroscopy, kinematics, IFU + datacubes (Morelli + Cassata).
- **late weeks**: SMBHs, ISM, dark matter, scaling relations.

labs (11/3, 22/4, 12-13/5): hands-on work with SDSS + MUSE + galaxy mass + datacubes.

## what this course is NOT

- it is not OC: the cosmological backdrop is assumed.
- it is not stellar astrophysics: stellar evolution is assumed.
- it is not ISM physics: that's the job of the spectroscopy + lab high-energy courses.

what's distinctive is the **bridge**: connecting the photometric / spectroscopic observables to the **physical model** of a galaxy.

## the big results

what students should be able to defend by exam day:
1. **galaxy bimodality**: red sequence vs blue cloud; what drives the divide.
2. **morphology-color-mass-environment** correlations.
3. **scaling relations**: Tully-Fisher (spirals), Faber-Jackson + Fundamental Plane (ellipticals).
4. **dark matter** in spirals (rotation curves), ellipticals (X-ray + tracers), dwarfs (high $M/L$), clusters (lensing + virial).
5. **SMBH masses** from reverberation mapping, stellar dynamics, water masers.
6. **M-σ + Magorrian relations** linking SMBH to bulge.
7. **stellar populations** of ellipticals: old, metal-rich, $\alpha$-enhanced.
8. **evolution**: cosmic SFR (Madau-Dickinson), galaxy mass build-up, downsizing, quenching.
