title: "Magnesium and alkali earths"
---

**alkali-earth atoms** (Be, Mg, Ca, Sr, Ba) have a closed-shell noble-gas core plus two valence $s$-electrons. their ground state is $^1S_0$ (paired spins). first excited states involve $sp$ configurations with both singlet and triplet branches.

## magnesium structure

Mg ($Z = 12$): configuration $[Ne] 3s^2$. ground $1\,^1S_0^e$.

key excited states:
| configuration | terms | first wavelength |
|---|---|---|
| $3s^1 3p^1$ | $3\,^1P_1^o$ + $3\,^3P_{0,1,2}^o$ | various |
| $3s^1 4s^1$ | $4\,^1S_0^e$ + $4\,^3S_1^e$ |  |
| $3s^1 3d^1$ | $3\,^1D_2^e$ + $3\,^3D_{1,2,3}^e$ |  |

## the b-triplet at $\sim 5170$ Å

the famous **Mg I b-triplet** comes from $3p\,^3P^o \to 3s\,^3S^e$ (a triplet-triplet permitted transition):
- $b_1$: $3\,^3P_2^o \to 3\,^3S_1^e$, $\lambda = 5167.32$ Å.
- $b_2$: $3\,^3P_1^o \to 3\,^3S_1^e$, $\lambda = 5172.68$ Å.
- $b_3$: $3\,^3P_0^o \to 3\,^3S_1^e$, $\lambda = 5183.60$ Å.

the three lines span $\sim 16$ Å, prominent in stars cooler than $\sim$ F0 (G, K, M). very strong in solar spectra. used as a temperature/gravity indicator and in old-stellar-population studies.

## the resonance line at $\lambda 2852$

Mg I $3\,^1P_1^o \to 3\,^1S_0^e$, the singlet equivalent of b-triplet, lies in the UV at $2852.13$ Å. seen as absorption in HST spectra of stellar atmospheres and ISM.

## Mg II h+k

removing one electron gives Mg$^+$ ($3s^1$ ground, hydrogenic on Mg core). its strong UV doublet:
- **Mg II h**: $3\,^2P_{1/2} \to 3\,^2S_{1/2}$, $\lambda = 2802.71$ Å.
- **Mg II k**: $3\,^2P_{3/2} \to 3\,^2S_{1/2}$, $\lambda = 2796.35$ Å.

key diagnostic in:
- ISM absorption along quasar sight lines (Mg II absorbers at $z = 0.4$ to $2.5$).
- AGN broad-line region.
- chromospheric activity (emission core in active stars).

## calcium

Ca ($Z = 20$): $[Ar] 4s^2$. ground $1\,^1S_0^e$. similar structure to Mg but the more important Ca features are from **Ca II**, the singly ionised stage.
