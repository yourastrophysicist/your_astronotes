# rotation curves

up: [Astrophysics_of_Galaxies_MOC](../../00_Atlas/Astrophysics_of_Galaxies_MOC.md) · [Dark matter rotation curves](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Dark matter rotation curves.md)

## summary

See the main note: [Dark matter rotation curves](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Dark matter rotation curves.md).

Galaxy rotation curves measure circular orbital velocity $V_c(R) = \sqrt{R rac{d\Phi}{dR}}$ of stars and gas as a function of galactocentric radius $R$. The observation of flat rotation curves out to large radii beyond the optical disk provides direct kinematic proof for massive, extended dark matter halos.
