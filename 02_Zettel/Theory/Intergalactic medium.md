the **intergalactic medium (IGM)** is the diffuse gas that fills the space between galaxies, containing most of the cosmic baryons. tracks the cosmological structure formation + reionisation history. observable through quasar absorption spectra.

## the components

the IGM is a multi-phase medium:
- **photoionised** (warm, $\sim 10^4$ K): the bulk at $z = 0$ to $5$. ionised by UV background.
- **shock-heated** (warm-hot, $\sim 10^5$ to $10^7$ K): the **WHIM**, hosting the missing baryons.
- **dense filaments** (cold, $\sim 10^4$ K, but with embedded clumps): trace LSS.
- **voids** (very low density, $\sim 10^{-7}$ cm$^{-3}$ baryons).

at $z \gtrsim 6$: significant **neutral H** (pre-reionisation).

## the baryon census

| component | fraction of $\Omega_b$ |
|---|---|
| stars | $\sim 7\%$ |
| ICM (cluster gas) | $\sim 4\%$ |
| WHIM | $\sim 50\%$ |
| photoionised IGM | $\sim 30\%$ |
| neutral hydrogen + cold molecular | $\sim 10\%$ |

so most baryons are **in the IGM**, not in galaxies. this distribution is a major prediction of cosmological simulations.

## the photoionised IGM

at $z = 0$ to $5$: most of the IGM is photoionised by the **UV background** from galaxies + AGN. equilibrium $T \sim 10^4$ K from photoionisation heating + collisional cooling.

observable through:
- **Lyman-α forest** in QSO sight lines: residual neutral H absorption tracks IGM density + temperature.
- **higher Lyman lines**: Ly-β, Ly-γ.
- **metal absorption lines**: C IV, Mg II, O VI tracers.

see Lyman-α forest.

## the WHIM (warm-hot intergalactic medium)

at $T \sim 10^5$ to $10^7$ K, hosted in **filaments connecting galaxies**, the WHIM is the "missing baryon" reservoir. observable through:
- **soft X-ray absorption** lines (O VII, O VIII, Ne IX) in QSO + AGN spectra.
- **SZ effect** integrated along sight lines.
- **redshift-space + Tracking IGM by tomography** in galaxy surveys.

XMM-Newton + Chandra detected hints; **eROSITA** + future X-ray missions (Athena) will map systematically.

see [Missing baryons](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Missing baryons.md).

## the cosmic web

the IGM is structured as a **cosmic web**: filaments connecting galaxy clusters, with vast voids in between. simulations (Millennium, IllustrisTNG) reproduce this structure from $\Lambda$CDM initial conditions.

filament gas: warm, low-density, contributes most of the IGM mass. cluster cores: hot, dense, contributes most of the X-ray emission.

## the chemical evolution

IGM metallicity at low $z$: $Z \sim 0.1\,Z_\odot$ near galaxies, much lower in voids. enriched by galactic outflows.

at high $z$: IGM is much more pristine, $Z \sim 10^{-3}\,Z_\odot$ at $z \sim 6$, primordial at $z \gtrsim 10$.

so the IGM has a strong **metallicity gradient** with redshift, tracking the rate of chemical enrichment by stellar nucleosynthesis + galactic feedback.

## observational tools

major IGM probes:
- **QSO absorption spectroscopy**: SDSS, BOSS, eBOSS, DESI. Ly-α forest + metal lines.
- **diffuse X-ray emission**: from cluster ICM + WHIM.
- **galaxy-IGM cross-correlations**: track the gas around galaxies.
- **21 cm tomography** (future): Nabokov + SKA. trace neutral H at high $z$.
- **fast radio bursts (FRBs)**: dispersion measure probes electron column density along sight lines.
