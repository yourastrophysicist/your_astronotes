**gravitational lensing** is the bending of light by gravity, predicted by GR and observed routinely. it makes massive foreground objects act as **gravitational lenses** that distort and magnify background sources. central tool of modern cosmology, dark-matter mapping, and galaxy formation.

## the basic physics

light bending by mass $M$ at impact parameter $b$ (Einstein 1915):
$$\Delta\phi = \frac{4GM}{c^2 b}$$

twice the Newtonian-expected value due to gravitational time dilation + spatial curvature contributing equally.

for an extended mass distribution at distance $d_L$ from observer + $d_{LS}$ from source, with angular separation $\beta$ (true source position) and $\theta$ (apparent image position), the lens equation:
$$\beta = \theta - \alpha(\theta)$$
with $\alpha(\theta)$ the deflection angle due to all the mass.

## three lensing regimes

### strong lensing
mass concentration is high enough to produce **multiple images** of the same background source, possibly with **arcs** or **rings** ("Einstein ring" if perfectly aligned).

requires $\Sigma > \Sigma_{\rm crit} \sim 2.5$ kg/m$^2$ at typical galaxy distances. occurs in galaxy clusters + dense individual galaxies.

### weak lensing
mass distribution is **diffuse** + the deflection is small ($\Delta\theta \ll \theta_{\rm source}$). produces small **shape distortions** of background galaxies (~$1\%$ ellipticity changes), not multiple images.

statistical: average shape distortions over many galaxies map the mass distribution.

### micro-lensing
a single compact object (planet, dark-matter MACHO) crosses the line of sight to a background star. brief brightening as the lens transits.

## the deflection in cosmology

a photon travelling through cosmic structure on its way from a source at $d_S$ to the observer is **continuously deflected** by intervening matter. the **convergence** $\kappa$ of light bundles encodes the matter distribution.

modern cosmological weak lensing surveys (DES, KiDS, HSC, Euclid) measure $\kappa$ statistics and constrain $\Omega_m, \sigma_8$, dark energy.

## the EHT image of M87 + Sgr A$^\star$

a special case: photon orbits around a black hole. the **photon sphere** at $r = 3GM$ produces a **bright ring** in EHT images (M87 in 2019, Sgr A$^\star$ in 2022). photons can complete one or more orbits before escaping, giving a sharp ring around the BH shadow.

see [Photon sphere](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Photon sphere.html).

## historical milestones

- **1915**: Einstein predicts $\Delta\phi = 4GM/(c^2 b)$.
- **1919**: Eddington's solar eclipse expedition measures $1.61''$ for solar limb stars, confirms GR.
- **1936**: Einstein notes lensing rings (Mandl had asked about them).
- **1979**: first lensed quasar, Q0957+561 (twin quasar).
- **1986**: discovery of arcs in clusters (Abell 370).
- **1988**: first Einstein ring.
- **2014-2016**: dark-energy weak-lensing surveys (DES) begin precision cosmology.
- **2019**: EHT image of M87.
