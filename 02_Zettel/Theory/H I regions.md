**HI regions** (also called neutral hydrogen regions) are zones of mostly atomic, non-ionised hydrogen surrounding HII regions or filling the diffuse ISM. cold ($T \sim 50$ to $100$ K), traceable via 21 cm emission and absorption.

## the physical conditions

beyond the Strömgren radius, photon flux drops to negligible and HII transitions to HI:
- **temperature**: $T \sim 100$ K (cold neutral medium, CNM) or $T \sim 8000$ K (warm neutral medium, WNM). both phases coexist in pressure balance.
- **density**: CNM $n \sim 30$ cm$^{-3}$; WNM $n \sim 0.5$ cm$^{-3}$.
- **ionisation**: H mostly neutral, with $x_e \sim 10^{-4}$ from cosmic-ray ionisation and metal photoionisation.
- **molecules**: H$_2$ forms in the densest parts (precursor to molecular clouds).

## tracers

### 21 cm emission

the spin-flip transition of neutral H, $\lambda = 21.1$ cm, $\nu = 1420$ MHz. permitted but very weak ($A_{21} = 2.85 \times 10^{-15}$ s$^{-1}$, lifetime $\sim 11$ Myr per atom). bright in HI clouds because of long path lengths and high column densities.

surveys: HI4PI all-sky, GBT, Arecibo, ATCA, ASKAP. observable from ground at radio bands. extragalactic HI traced to $z \sim 0.5$ with current facilities, $z \sim 1$ with SKA-1.

### Lyman alpha absorption

UV $\lambda 1215.7$ Å. seen in absorption against background sources. at high $z$, the **Lyman alpha forest** (a forest of Ly$\alpha$ absorbers along QSO sight lines) is the dominant baryon-tracing tool, mapping HI in the IGM at $z \sim 2$ to $5$.

### neutral metal absorption lines

Na I D, Ca II H+K in absorption against background stars. trace cold diffuse HI clouds in the Galactic disk.

### absorption against radio continuum

21 cm absorption against a bright radio source can probe HI at high column density.

## boundary with HII

at the Strömgren-sphere boundary, the ionisation transitions sharply from $\sim 100\%$ to $\sim 0\%$. just outside, in the **photodissociation region (PDR)**, atoms remain mostly neutral but UV photons can dissociate molecules and excite atomic levels. PDRs emit:
- $[CII] \lambda 158\,\mu$m
- $[OI] \lambda 63\,\mu$m
- $H_2$ rotational lines

bright in dense star-forming regions (e.g. Orion). see [Photodissociation regions PDRs](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Photodissociation regions PDRs.html).

## the Galactic HI distribution

the Milky Way's HI:
- thin disk at $|z| < 100$ pc, density $\sim 0.5$ cm$^{-3}$ averaged.
- thick layer extending to $|z| \sim 1$ kpc.
- spiral arms and shell structures from supernova feedback.
- mass: $\sim 5 \times 10^9\,M_\odot$ in the Galactic disk.

similar mass to molecular gas. dominant baryonic component of the cold ISM.

## the HI mass function

extragalactic HI surveys (HIPASS, ALFALFA) give the HI mass function: distribution of galaxies' HI content. similar to but distinct from the stellar mass function. correlates with morphology (spirals are HI-rich, ellipticals HI-poor).
