**$\Lambda$CDM** is the **standard model of cosmology**: a flat universe with cold dark matter + cosmological constant + standard inflation initial conditions. fits all current cosmological data with **6 parameters**.

## the 6 base parameters

(from Planck 2018 + BAO):

| parameter | symbol | value |
|---|---|---|
| baryon density | $\Omega_b h^2$ | $0.0224 \pm 0.0001$ |
| cold dark matter density | $\Omega_c h^2$ | $0.120 \pm 0.001$ |
| Hubble | $H_0$ (or $h$) | $67.4 \pm 0.5$ km/s/Mpc |
| optical depth to reionisation | $\tau$ | $0.054 \pm 0.007$ |
| primordial scalar amplitude | $\ln(10^{10} A_s)$ | $3.04 \pm 0.01$ |
| spectral index | $n_s$ | $0.965 \pm 0.004$ |

## the derived parameters

| parameter | value |
|---|---|
| total matter $\Omega_m$ | $0.315 \pm 0.007$ |
| dark energy $\Omega_\Lambda$ | $0.685 \pm 0.007$ |
| spatial curvature $|\Omega_k|$ | $< 0.005$ (flat) |
| age of universe $t_0$ | $13.797 \pm 0.023$ Gyr |
| sound horizon at recombination $r_d$ | $147.6 \pm 0.2$ Mpc |
| matter-radiation equality $z_{\rm eq}$ | $3402 \pm 26$ |
| reionisation $z_{\rm reion}$ | $7.7 \pm 0.6$ |

## the energy budget

today:
- **5%** baryons (visible matter: stars, gas, dust).
- **27%** dark matter (cold, non-baryonic, gravitationally interacting).
- **68%** dark energy (cosmological constant or close).

so **95% of the universe is dark**, only known through gravity.

## what $\Lambda$CDM fits

a single parameter set fits an extraordinary range of observations:
- **CMB power spectrum**: position + heights of acoustic peaks (Planck).
- **BAO** in galaxy clustering at $z = 0$ to $2$ (DESI, BOSS).
- **SN Ia Hubble diagram** at $z = 0$ to $1.5$ (Pantheon).
- **galaxy clustering + weak lensing** at $z = 0$ to $2$ (DES, KiDS).
- **Lyman-α forest** in QSO spectra at $z = 2$ to $5$.
- **galaxy abundance + halo mass function** at $z = 0$ to $7$.
- **abundance of light elements** from BBN at $z \sim 10^9$.
- **SZ cluster counts** in CMB surveys.

agreement at $\sim 1\%$ precision across all of these is **the** triumph of modern cosmology.

## the open questions

despite the success, several tensions:
- **Hubble tension**: SH0ES local $H_0 = 73$ vs Planck $H_0 = 67.4$, $5\sigma$ disagreement.
- **$\sigma_8$ tension**: lensing surveys (DES, KiDS) prefer slightly lower clustering amplitude than Planck.
- **DESI 2024-2025 BAO**: hint at $w \ne -1$ for dark energy, unresolved.

these may indicate new physics or unidentified systematics. still under investigation.

## extensions to $\Lambda$CDM

if $\Lambda$CDM doesn't fit perfectly, what extensions might:
- **massive neutrinos** $\sum m_\nu$: contributes to matter density, affects power spectrum.
- **dynamical dark energy** $w(z)$: a different equation of state.
- **non-zero spatial curvature** $\Omega_k$: still observationally allowed at $|\Omega_k| < 0.005$.
- **early dark energy**: a transient component near recombination, helps with $H_0$ tension.
- **modified gravity**: deviations from GR on large scales.
- **non-standard recombination history**.

each extension tests different physical models against the data.
