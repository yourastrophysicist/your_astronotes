# starburst galaxies

up: [Pablo_03_Star_formation_in_galaxies](../../01_Literature/Lectures/Observational_Cosmology/Pablo_03_Star_formation_in_galaxies.md)

## what makes a starburst

a starburst is a galaxy where the current SFR is much higher than its long-term average, typically sSFR $\gtrsim 10^{-9}$ yr$^{-1}$ (gas-depletion time $< 1$ Gyr). the burst is **transient**: a galaxy cannot sustain it for more than $\sim 100$ Myr before running out of gas.

from [Galaxy time scales](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Galaxy time scales.md): starburst $t_{SF} \equiv M_{\text{gas}}/\psi$ is $\sim 100$ Myr, comparable to or shorter than a dynamical time. so starbursts are self-quenching.

## the prototype: M82

the canonical nearby starburst. at $d = 3.5$ Mpc:

- $\psi \sim 10\,M_\odot/\text{yr}$
- nuclear, on $\sim 1$ kpc scale
- driven by tidal interaction with M81
- drives a bipolar superwind observable in Hα, X-rays, and sub-mm CO (Herschel SPIRE shows CO ladder up to J=13)
- dusty: most of its bolometric output emerges as IR

## related classes

- **Wolf-Rayet galaxies**: host a detectable population of W-R stars (He-burning, high-mass-loss, very young < 5 Myr burst). observable as broad He II 4686 Å and C IV 5808 Å emission blended with the Balmer series. a purely-spectroscopic class
- **HII galaxies / BCDs (blue compact dwarfs)**: small, gas-rich, metal-poor, dominated at optical wavelengths by recent SF. may hide older populations detectable only in NIR. they are natural local analogues of high-$z$ SFGs
- **green peas**: a modern subclass (Cardamone 2009), extremely compact + strong [OIII] 5007 emission

## observational signatures

- strong nebular emission: Hα, Hβ, [OII], [OIII], [NeIII], HeI, [SII]
- UV-blue continuum from O/B stars
- mid-IR: PAH bands (Spitzer IRS of M82 shows the 3–40 μm SED dominated by PAH and silicate features)
- far-IR: cold-dust peak near 60–100 μm
- superwind features: hot X-ray plasma, biconical Hα, blueshifted absorption lines (Na D, Mg II)
- frequent signs of interaction / tidal disturbance

## feedback: superwinds, SSCs, shocks

intense SF drives:
- **superstar clusters (SSCs)**: compact $M_* \gtrsim 10^5\,M_\odot$ clusters at ages $< 10$ Myr. ionized bubbles around them. NGC 5461 example in Pablo's slide
- **bubbles and superwinds**: blown by cumulative SNII energy, escaping the disk. can remove a large fraction of ISM
- **propagating SF**: shock fronts from SNe compress nearby gas, triggering the next generation

## why these matter

- dominate the cosmic SFR at $z > 1$ via their ULIRG descendants (see [LIRGs ULIRGs HyLIRGs](../../02_Zettel/Theory/LIRGs ULIRGs HyLIRGs.md))
- drive metal enrichment of the CGM and IGM
- are laboratories for extreme SF physics, testing the IMF, SF laws, feedback models
- are the visible-universe analogue of the first galaxies
