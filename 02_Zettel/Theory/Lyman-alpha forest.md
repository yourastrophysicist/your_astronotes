title: "Lyman-alpha forest"
---

the **Lyman-α forest** is a forest of narrow Lyman-α absorption lines in the spectra of high-$z$ quasars, produced by neutral H clouds + IGM along the line of sight. one of the most powerful probes of the IGM + LSS at $z = 2$ to $5$.

## the basic picture

a quasar at $z_{\rm QSO}$ emits a continuum spectrum + Ly-α emission line at $1216$ Å rest-frame. the photons travel through the IGM. each cloud of neutral H along the way absorbs photons that are at the cloud's local Ly-α wavelength: $1216(1 + z_{\rm cloud})$ Å observed.

so as the photons climb through the IGM, they pass through many H clouds at decreasing $z$. each absorbs at a slightly different observed wavelength. the result: a **forest** of absorption lines blueward of the QSO's Ly-α emission line.

## the spectrum

a Ly-α forest spectrum looks like:
- broad QSO emission features at $z_{\rm QSO}$ rest-frame.
- a forest of narrow absorption lines at $\lambda < 1216(1 + z_{\rm QSO})$ Å observed, corresponding to clouds at $z_{\rm cloud} < z_{\rm QSO}$.
- redward of the Ly-α emission, the spectrum is clear (no Ly-α absorbers redward).

each absorption line gives a **column density** $N_{\rm HI}$ + **redshift** $z_{\rm cloud}$ + **velocity width** $b$.

## the column-density distribution

absorbers come in three populations:
- **Ly-α forest**: $N_{\rm HI} = 10^{12}$ to $10^{17}$ cm$^{-2}$. the bulk. trace IGM.
- **Lyman-limit systems (LLS)**: $N_{\rm HI} = 10^{17}$ to $10^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$. dense knots + galactic-scale absorbers. self-shielded.
- **Damped Lyman alpha (DLA)**: $N_{\rm HI} > 10^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$. galaxy-scale neutral H, e.g. proto-disks.

the **column density distribution** $f(N_{\rm HI}) \propto N_{\rm HI}^{-1.5}$ approximately. consistent with cosmological simulations + structure formation.

## what the forest teaches

### IGM ionisation
ratio of neutral to ionised H along sight lines: $n_{\rm HI}/n_{\rm tot} \sim 10^{-5}$ at $z \sim 3$, increasing toward $z = 6$. signs the universe is mostly ionised at $z < 6$ but transitions to neutral at $z \sim 6$ (Gunn-Peterson trough).

### IGM temperature
voigt profiles of Ly-α lines give the **velocity width** $b \sim 20$ to $30$ km/s, dominated by **thermal broadening** at $T \sim 10^4$ K, the photoionisation-equilibrium temperature.

### IGM density distribution
at $z \sim 2$ to $5$, the IGM is structured: density fluctuations + filaments. the forest's **transmission flux fluctuations** trace IGM density.

### cosmological constraints
small-scale matter power spectrum at $z \sim 2$ to $5$, $k \sim 1$ to $10$ Mpc$^{-1}$. constraint on neutrino mass + warm dark matter (which suppress small-scale power).

### the BAO at $z \sim 2$
BOSS + eBOSS used Ly-α correlations to detect the BAO peak in the IGM at $z \approx 2.4$. independent confirmation of $\Lambda$CDM at high $z$.

## the Gunn-Peterson trough

at $z \gtrsim 6$, the universe was substantially neutral. the Ly-α optical depth becomes $\tau \gg 1$, completely blocking the spectrum blueward of $1216$ Å rest-frame. observed in QSOs at $z > 6$ (SDSS, ULAS): $> 99\%$ absorption.

so the Gunn-Peterson trough marks the **end of the reionisation epoch**.

## the metal-line forest

besides Ly-α, the IGM clouds have metal absorption lines (C IV, Mg II, O VI). these:
- track **chemical enrichment** of the IGM by galactic outflows.
- provide ionisation diagnostics (different ions in different temperature regimes).
- map circumgalactic medium (CGM) around foreground galaxies.
