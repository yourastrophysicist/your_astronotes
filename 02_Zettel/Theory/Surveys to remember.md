# surveys to remember

up: [Observational_Cosmology_MOC](../../00_Atlas/Observational_Cosmology_MOC.html) · [Astrophysics_of_Galaxies_MOC](../../00_Atlas/Astrophysics_of_Galaxies_MOC.html)

## wide-field optical / spectroscopic surveys

- **SDSS (Sloan Digital Sky Survey)**:
  - Apache Point Observatory 2.5m telescope.
  - 5-band imaging ($u, g, r, i, z$) over $14{,}500\text{ deg}^2$.
  - Multi-fiber spectroscopy ($> 3$ million astronomical spectra).
  - Benchmark for the local galaxy luminosity function, color bimodality, and LSS.
- **2dFGRS (Two-degree-Field Galaxy Redshift Survey)**:
  - Anglo-Australian Telescope (AAT) 3.9m.
  - $\sim 220{,}000$ galaxy redshifts, early measurement of $\Omega_m \approx 0.3$ and first detection of BAO.
- **BOSS / eBOSS (Baryon Oscillation Spectroscopic Survey)**:
  - SDSS-III and SDSS-IV; mapped $> 1.5$ million galaxies and quasars out to $z \sim 2.2$.
  - Precision BAO scale measurements anchoring cosmological distances.
- **DESI (Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument)**:
  - Mayall 4m telescope at Kitt Peak; 5000 robotic focal-plane positioners.
  - Spectroscopic survey of $\sim 40$ million galaxies and quasars over $14{,}000\text{ deg}^2$.
  - 2024 results: highest precision BAO constraints across $z = 0.1 - 4.2$.

## wide-field imaging surveys

- **DES (Dark Energy Survey)**:
  - Blanco 4m telescope at CTIO (DECam, 570-megapixel CCD).
  - $5000\text{ deg}^2$ in $g, r, i, z, Y$.
  - Combined weak lensing shear, galaxy clustering, and Type Ia supernovae.
- **Rubin Observatory LSST (Legacy Survey of Space and Time)**:
  - Simonyi Survey Telescope 8.4m, Cerro Pachón, Chile.
  - 3.2-gigapixel camera imaging $18{,}000\text{ deg}^2$ every 3-4 nights for 10 years.
  - 20 billion galaxies; probes dark matter, dark energy, and transient astrophysics.

## space cosmological surveys

- **COBE, WMAP, Planck**:
  - All-sky CMB missions. Planck (2009-2013): ultimate full-sky temperature and polarization power spectra.
- **HST Deep Fields**:
  - HDF (1995), HUDF (2004), CANDELS, Frontier Fields (lensing clusters).
- **Euclid (ESA)**:
  - 1.2m space telescope launched 2023.
  - $15{,}000\text{ deg}^2$ survey in optical ($VIS$) and NIR ($NISP$).
  - Primary probes: weak lensing cosmic shear and galaxy clustering (BAO).
- **Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope (NASA)**:
  - 2.4m wide-field infrared space telescope ($100\times$ HST field of view).
  - High Latitude Wide Area Survey for cosmology, dark energy, and microlensing.
