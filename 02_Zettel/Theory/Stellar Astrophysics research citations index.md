## Block 0-1 — Reading the CMD

**foundational SSP work:**
- **Renzini & Buzzoni 1986** — historical definition of SSPs; GCs as old SSPs, OCs as young SSPs.

**Milone's own papers as research backbone:**
- **Milone et al. 2025, A&A 696, 221 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Milone_2025_NGC6397_CMD.pdf)** — modern reference for the CMD evolutionary sequences (used as reference plot in Lecture 1).
- **Milone et al. 2017, MNRAS 464, 3636 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Milone_2017_HST_UV_Legacy.pdf)** — HST UV Legacy Survey of Galactic GCs: the foundational paper for chromosome maps + Type I/II classification of $\sim 60$ GCs.

**isochrone libraries:**
- **PARSEC** (Bressan et al. 2012, Padova).
- **DSEP / Dartmouth** (Dotter et al. 2008).
- **BaSTI** (Pietrinferni et al. 2004, 2006).

## Block 2 — interstellar reddening

- **Cardelli, Clayton, Mathis 1989, ApJ 345, 245** — the standard CCM extinction law parametrisation, $R_V = 3.1$ in diffuse ISM.
- **Schlegel, Finkbeiner, Davis 1998 (SFD)** — full-sky $E(B-V)$ map from IRAS + DIRBE.
- **Schlafly & Finkbeiner 2011** — recalibration of SFD using SDSS stellar colours.

---

## Block 3 — Galaxy formation from CMD

- **Dotter et al. 2011, ApJ 738, 74 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Dotter_2011_GCs_AgeMetallicity.pdf)** — age-metallicity relation of $61$ Galactic GCs, separating in-situ vs accreted populations.
- **Searle & Zinn 1978** — first dual-origin model for Galactic GC system.
- **Marin-Franch et al. 2009** — relative GC ages.
- **Forbes & Bridges 2010** — accreted vs in-situ GC division.
- **Helmi et al. 2018** — Gaia-Enceladus discovery.
- **Belokurov et al. 2018** — "Sausage" structure in Gaia.
- **Massari, Koppelman, Helmi 2019** — origin of GCs based on Gaia kinematics.

---

## Block 4 — ultra-faint dwarf galaxies

- **Willman et al. 2005** — Willman 1, first SDSS UFDG.
- **Belokurov et al. 2007** — five new UFDGs from SDSS.
- **Frebel & Norris 2015 review** — chemical signatures of UFDGs.
- **Simon 2019 review** — kinematics + DM content of dwarfs.
- **Ji et al. 2016** — r-process enhancement in Reticulum II.

## Block 5 — white dwarfs

- **Hansen et al. 2007, ApJ 671, 380 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Hansen_2007_NGC6397_WDCS.pdf)** — WD cooling sequence age of NGC 6397: $t_{\rm WDCS} = 11.47 \pm 0.47$ Gyr.
- **Mestel 1952** — analytic WD cooling theory.
- **Chandrasekhar 1931** — mass limit derivation.
- **Cassisi (private comm.)** — simulated WD luminosity function used in lectures.
- **Salaris & Cassisi 2006** review on WDs.
- **Tremblay et al. 2019** — modern WD physics review.

## Block 6 — stellar spectroscopy

- **Saha 1920** — ionisation equation.
- **Boltzmann distribution** — excitation balance.
- **Gray, *The Observation and Analysis of Stellar Photospheres*** — standard spectroscopy textbook.
- **Asplund 2005 review** — solar abundances + NLTE.
- **Cayrel et al. 2004** — abundances of metal-poor halo giants.

- **Lagioia et al. 2014, ApJ 782, 50** — high-resolution DR map for Baade Window via NGC 6528.

## Block 8 — binaries, blue stragglers, exotic objects

- **Sandage 1953** — first blue straggler discovery in M3.
- **Hills & Day 1976** — collisional formation of BSs.
- **McCrea 1964** — binary mass transfer formation of BSs.
- **Ferraro et al. 2009, Nature 462, 483** — Terzan 5 two metal populations: a fossil bulge fragment?
- **Massari et al. 2014** — Terzan 5 metallicity distribution function.
- **Milone et al. 2012, A&A 540, A16 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Milone_2012_DR_Binaries.pdf)** — binary fractions in 59 GCs.
- **Sollima et al. 2007** — binary fraction in NGC 5466.

**X-ray + MSPs:**
- **Heinke et al. 2003, 2005** — Chandra X-ray sources in 47 Tuc.
- **Ransom et al. 2005** — 21 MSPs in Terzan 5.

## Block 9 — stellar mass function + black holes

- **Salpeter 1955, ApJ 121, 161** — original IMF, $\xi(M) \propto M^{-2.35}$.
- **Kroupa 2001, MNRAS 322, 231 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Kroupa_2001_IMF.pdf)** — broken power-law IMF.
- **Chabrier 2003, PASP 115, 763 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Chabrier_2003_IMF.pdf)** — lognormal IMF below 1 $M_\odot$.
- **Giesers et al. 2018, MNRAS 475, L15** — first stellar BH detected via radial velocities in NGC 3201 ($\sim 4 M_\odot$).
- **Strader et al. 2012** — radio BH candidates in M22.
- **van der Marel & Anderson 2010** — IMBH constraints in $\omega$ Cen.

## Block 11 — multiple populations in GCs

**Milone's signature topic. extensive citation list.**

**spectroscopic discovery:**
- **Carretta & Gratton 1990s** — Na-O anti-correlation discovery via FLAMES + UVES.
- **Carretta et al. 2009** — comprehensive Na-O survey in 19 GCs.
- **Norris 1981** — Mg-Al variations.
- **Yong et al. 2003, 2008** — CN-CH anti-correlations.

**photometric MPs:**
- **Bedin et al. 2004 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Bedin_2004_omegaCen_SplitMS.pdf)** — split MS in $\omega$ Cen, the first photometric MP detection.
- **Piotto et al. 2007 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Piotto_2007_NGC2808_TripleMS.pdf)** — triple MS in NGC 2808.
- **Milone et al. 2008** — split MS in NGC 6752.
- **Anderson et al. 2009** — multiple SGBs.
- **Milone et al. 2012, A&A 540, A16 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Milone_2012_DR_Binaries.pdf)** — population fractions in $\sim 20$ GCs.

**chromosome maps:**
- **Milone et al. 2015, MNRAS 455, 3009 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Milone_2015_NGC2808_MPs.pdf)** — first chromosome maps for NGC 2808.
- **Milone et al. 2017, MNRAS 464, 3636 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Milone_2017_HST_UV_Legacy.pdf)** — HST UV Legacy Survey: chromosome maps for $\sim 60$ GCs; Type I/II classification.
- **Marino, Milone et al. 2019 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Marino_2019_MPs_Spectroscopy.pdf)** — chemical patterns on chromosome maps.
- **Marino et al. 2009, 2011** — M22, NGC 1851 multi-iron populations.

**polluter scenarios:**
- **Decressin et al. 2007** — fast rotating massive star (FRMS) model.
- **D'Ercole et al. 2008** — AGB pollution model.
- **de Mink et al. 2009** — interacting binary model.
- **Denissenkov & Hartwick 2014** — supermassive star model.

## Block 12 — extended main sequence phenomenon

- **Mackey & Broby Nielsen 2007 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Mackey_2007_NGC1846_eMSTO.pdf)** — first eMSTO discovery in NGC 1846.

- **Bastian & de Mink 2009 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Bastian_2009_eMSTO_Rotation.pdf)** — rotation as origin of eMSTO.

- **Milone et al. 2018 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Milone_2018_NGC1850_eMSTO.pdf)** — split upper MS in NGC 1755 + NGC 1850.
- **Marino et al. 2018** — slow + fast rotators in NGC 1866.
- **Niederhofer et al. 2015** — age spread interpretation.
- **D'Antona et al. 2015** — rotation $\to$ He variation argument.

## Block 13 — the Bulge

- **Zoccali et al. 2003, A&A 399, 931** — bulge as old as GCs from CMD analysis.
- **Clarkson et al. 2008, ApJ 684, 1110 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Clarkson_2008_Bulge_CMD.pdf)** — HST PM-cleaned bulge CMD: $< 5\%$ stars younger than 5 Gyr.
- **Valenti et al. 2013, A&A 559, A98** — statistical decontamination of bulge field via reference field subtraction.
- **Lagioia et al. 2014, ApJ 782, 50** — high-resolution differential-reddening map for Baade Window via NGC 6528 cluster ridge; bulge age $10$-$12$ Gyr.
- **Renzini et al. 2018, ApJ 863, 16** — five-band HST photometry + reddening-free pseudo-colour diagrams across five bulge fields (SWEEPS, Baade, Stanek, OGLE-29); bulk old, $\sim 3\%$ younger than 5 Gyr.
- **Bensby et al. 2013, A&A 549, A147** — high-resolution UVES spectra of $\sim 60$ microlensed bulge dwarfs; age-metallicity from $\log g$-$\log T_{\rm eff}$ + isochrones.
- **Bensby et al. 2017, A&A 605, A89 [\[PDF\]](file:///Users/sxafq/Documents/yourastro_notes/02_Literature/Papers/Bensby_2017_Bulge_Microlensing.pdf)** — extended sample to $\sim 90$ microlensed dwarfs; $\sim 30\%$ younger than 7 Gyr; **two-component bulge** evidence.
- **Alves-Brito et al. 2010** — $[\alpha/{\rm Fe}]$ in bulge giants.
- **Gonzalez et al. 2011** — ARGOS/GIBS metallicity survey of bulge.
- **Ness et al. 2013** — APOGEE/ARGOS five bulge populations from MDF.
- **Johnson et al. 2013** — bulge globular cluster abundance trends.
- **Matteucci & Romano 1999** — chemical evolution model favouring fast bulge formation.
- **Ferraro et al. 2009, Nature 462, 483** — Terzan 5 two metal populations, possibly fossil bulge fragment.
- **Massari et al. 2014, ApJ 795, 22** — Terzan 5 metallicity distribution function.

**bulge surveys:**
- **OGLE** (Udalski 1992-): millions of bulge light curves.
- **MOA, MACHO, EROS** — sister microlensing surveys.
- **APOGEE** (Majewski 2017, AJ 154, 94) — IR spectroscopy of $\sim 10^5$ bulge stars.
- **GIBS** (Zoccali 2014) — Giraffe Inner Bulge Survey.
- **ARGOS** (Freeman 2013) — Australian Galactic Bulge Survey.
- **VVV** (Minniti 2010) — VISTA Variables in the Via Lactea.

## Block 14 — the Galactic Disk

- **CMD constraints on disk vs halo populations** — comparing disk and halo turn-off ages and metallicity structures to map the disk growth history.

## Block 15 — the Second Parameter Problem

- **Milone et al. 2014, MNRAS 439, 1588**: established the $L_1$ and $L_2$ metrics for horizontal branch morphology.
- **Dotter et al. 2010, ApJ 708, 998**: age as the dominant global second parameter.
- **Recio-Blanco et al. 2006, A&A 452, 119**: maximum temperature/blue extension of the HB and its correlation with cluster mass.

---

## Block 16 — Dwarf Galaxies and Dark Matter

- **Simon 2019, ARA&A 57, 375** — kinematics and dark matter content of dwarf galaxies.
- **Brown et al. 2014, ApJ 796, 91** — reionization suppression of star formation in six UFDGs.
- **Muratore et al. 2026** — JWST and HST high-precision photometry of Boötes I.
- **Massari, Koppelman, Helmi 2019, A&A 630, A129** — origins of Galactic globular clusters using Gaia kinematics.

---

## key surveys cited across multiple lectures

- **HST UV Legacy Survey of Galactic GCs** — Piotto, Milone (PI), 2015 onward. backbone of the MP work.
- **Gaia DR2/DR3** (2018, 2022, 2023) — precision astrometry + photometry, critical for kinematic separation in dwarf galaxies + halo + bulge.
- **APOGEE** — IR multi-object spectrograph at APO + LCO; massive bulge + halo abundance database.
- **GALAH** — high-resolution survey of $\sim 10^6$ stars; MW chemical archaeology.
- **Pan-STARRS, DES, SDSS** — photometric surveys for UFDG discovery.
- **JWST** — first stars + extragalactic clusters at high $z$ (e.g., Sparkler galaxy at $z \sim 9$).
