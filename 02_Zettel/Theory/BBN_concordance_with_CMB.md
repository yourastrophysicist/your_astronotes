**both BBN and CMB anisotropies provide independent measurements of the baryon density** $\Omega_{0b}$. these are two completely independent windows on the same physics, and they agree to remarkable precision.

this concordance is one of the strongest validations of the standard hot Big Bang model we have.

---

## the two measurements

from **CMB** (Planck 2018):
$$\Omega_b h^2 = 0.0224 \pm 0.0001$$

from the height of the acoustic peaks in the CMB temperature power spectrum, which is set by the baryon-photon fluid dynamics before recombination.

![bbn-46](../../assets/images/bbn-46.png)

from **BBN** (Cooke et al. 2018, primarily from D/H in high-z quasar absorbers):
$$\Omega_b h^2 = 0.0224 \pm 0.0001$$

from the abundance of light elements forged when the universe was three minutes old.

---

## the concordance plot

a beautiful summary plot:

![bbn-47](../../assets/images/bbn-47.png)

(top) $Y_p^{\rm BBN}$ vs $\omega_b = \Omega_b h^2$, with the standard BBN prediction band (Aver et al. 2015 measurement bracketing the BBN curve) and the Planck CMB constraint (vertical red band).

(bottom) $y_{DP}$ = D/H vs $\omega_b$, with two theoretical predictions (Adelberger et al. 2011 vs Marcucci et al. 2016 nuclear rate determinations) and the Cooke et al. 2018 measurement.

both elements pin down $\omega_b$ at the *same* place that the CMB does, the vertical red band.

---

## why this matters

the two measurements are made:
- **at very different epochs** ($T \sim 0.1$ MeV vs $T \sim 0.3$ eV)
- **using completely different physics** (nuclear reaction rates vs sound waves in a photon-baryon fluid)
- **with completely different observational techniques** (light-element abundance spectroscopy vs anisotropy power spectrum analysis)

their agreement to 1% is therefore a non-trivial test of:
- the standard model of cosmology between BBN and recombination
- the assumption of a constant baryon-to-photon ratio over those decades of expansion
- nuclear cross-sections and weak interaction physics
- the structure formation framework that underlies CMB peak heights

---

## the broader story

this is the heart of why **BBN is one of the three observational pillars** of the standard hot Big Bang model, alongside:
1. the **Hubble diagram** (universe expanding)
2. the **CMB blackbody** (universe was hot)
3. **BBN** (universe at $T \sim 1$ MeV produced the observed light element abundances)

→ see [Cosmic_inventory_overview](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Cosmic_inventory_overview.md).

if any of these three were inconsistent with each other, our cosmological model would have problems. they are all consistent.

---

## the residual lithium problem

the one outstanding tension is in $^7$Li. CMB-anchored $\eta$ predicts $^7$Li/H about **a factor of 3 higher** than what is measured in metal-poor halo stars. possible explanations:
- stellar destruction of $^7$Li in the atmospheres of these stars (atomic diffusion + turbulence)
- uncertainties in the cross-sections for the reactions producing/destroying $^7$Li
- new physics (e.g. exotic dark matter relics, late-decaying particles)

this remains an active area of research. but unlike the Hubble tension or the dark energy problem, the lithium problem has not yet forced any major revision of the standard cosmological model. → see [BBN_observations](../../02_Zettel/Theory/BBN_observations.md).
