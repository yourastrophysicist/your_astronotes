title: "Phase transitions"

cosmic **phase transitions** mark moments when the symmetry of the early universe was **broken** as it cooled, producing the structure of fundamental forces + particles we see today. each transition leaves observational + theoretical relics.

## the major transitions

| transition | $T$ | $t$ | what changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planck era ends | $\sim 10^{19}$ GeV | $10^{-43}$ s | quantum gravity gives way to classical spacetime |
| GUT transition | $\sim 10^{16}$ GeV | $10^{-36}$ s | strong + electroweak forces differentiate |
| inflation ends | $\sim 10^{16}$ GeV | $10^{-32}$ s | reheating, Hot Big Bang begins |
| **electroweak transition** | $\sim 100$ GeV | $10^{-11}$ s | EM + weak forces differentiate; Higgs mechanism gives mass |
| **QCD transition** | $\sim 200$ MeV | $10^{-5}$ s | quarks confine into hadrons |
| BBN | $\sim 1$ MeV | $\sim 1$ to $20$ min | nuclei form |

## electroweak transition

at $T > 100$ GeV: EM + weak forces are **unified** as the electroweak (EW) interaction. Higgs field has zero VEV. all gauge bosons (W, Z, photon) are massless.

at $T \sim 100$ GeV: Higgs field acquires a non-zero VEV, $\langle H\rangle = 246$ GeV. **spontaneously breaks** EW symmetry. result:
- W + Z bosons acquire mass via the **Higgs mechanism**.
- photon remains massless.
- fermions acquire masses via Yukawa couplings.
- weak + EM forces differentiate.

modern view (LHC + lattice QCD): EW transition is a **smooth crossover**, not a sharp first-order transition. so no major cosmological remnants (like bubble walls or topological defects).

implications: can't easily generate **electroweak baryogenesis** (the conversion of matter-antimatter asymmetry happens too gently). need new physics beyond Standard Model for baryogenesis.

## QCD transition

at $T > 200$ MeV: quarks + gluons exist as a **deconfined plasma** ("quark-gluon plasma"). high-energy nuclear collisions at LHC + RHIC reproduce this state.

at $T \sim 200$ MeV: quarks + gluons **confine** into hadrons (protons, neutrons, mesons). the gas of hadrons is what we see today.

modern view (lattice QCD): QCD transition is also a **smooth crossover** at the standard quark masses + $T \sim 156$ MeV. no major cosmological signatures.

implications: contributes to the **chiral symmetry breaking** + the **vacuum condensate** of QCD. small corrections to BBN initial conditions.

## inflation + reheating

inflation ends with the inflaton field decaying into Standard Model particles, **reheating** the universe to $\sim 10^{15}$ GeV. this is also a phase transition (from quasi-de Sitter inflation to thermal radiation domination), but driven by inflaton dynamics.

after reheating: the universe is filled with thermalised relativistic particles, beginning the standard Hot Big Bang.

## GUT transition

speculative: at $T \sim 10^{16}$ GeV, hypothesised **Grand Unified Theories** (SU(5), SO(10)) predict the strong + electroweak forces unify into a single gauge symmetry, broken by a heavy Higgs.

predicted relics:
- **monopoles**: topological defects with $M_{\rm mono} \sim 10^{16}$ GeV.
- **baryon-number violation**: protons could decay (predicted lifetime $\sim 10^{34}$ yr in minimal SU(5), ruled out).

inflation **dilutes** any GUT relics to undetectable levels, providing one of its main motivations (the **monopole problem**).

## phase transitions + gravitational waves

**first-order phase transitions** produce gravitational waves via bubble nucleation + collision. observable today as a stochastic GW background. predicted signatures depend on the model.

current limits from PTAs (NANOGrav, EPTA): possible cosmological-phase-transition GW signal at $\sim$nHz frequency, debated. LISA + future GW detectors will probe this further.

if confirmed: a **direct probe** of physics beyond the Standard Model (e.g. dark phase transitions, hidden sectors).
