key cosmic epochs that mark transitions between qualitatively different regimes. these are the **landmarks** of cosmic history, useful for orienting any observational claim.

## the major transitions

| transition | $z$ | $T$ | age | what changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Planck era ends** | $\sim 10^{32}$ | $10^{32}$ K | $10^{-43}$ s | quantum gravity ends, classical spacetime begins |
| **inflation ends** | $\sim 10^{27}$ | $\sim 10^{16}$ GeV | $\sim 10^{-32}$ s | reheating; standard Hot Big Bang begins |
| **electroweak transition** | $\sim 10^{15}$ | $\sim 100$ GeV | $\sim 10^{-11}$ s | EW + EM differentiate |
| **QCD transition** | $\sim 10^{12}$ | $\sim 200$ MeV | $\sim 10^{-5}$ s | quarks confine into hadrons |
| **neutrino decoupling** | $\sim 10^{10}$ | $\sim 1$ MeV | $\sim 1$ s | neutrinos stop interacting |
| **$e^\pm$ annihilation** | $\sim 10^9$ | $\sim 0.5$ MeV | $\sim 30$ s | $T_\nu/T_\gamma = (4/11)^{1/3}$ established |
| **Big Bang nucleosynthesis** | $\sim 10^{9}$ to $10^8$ | $\sim 100$ keV | $\sim 1$ to $20$ min | $^4$He, D, $^7$Li synthesised |
| **matter-radiation equality** | $\sim 3400$ | $\sim 0.8$ eV | $\sim 50\,000$ yr | matter starts dominating |
| **photon decoupling** | $\sim 1100$ | $\sim 0.26$ eV | $\sim 380\,000$ yr | CMB released, photons free-stream |

## the conceptual bins

three big bins:

### early universe ($t < 100\,000$ yr, $z > 3400$)
**radiation-dominated**. $a \propto t^{1/2}$. plasma, opaque to photons. all action at high temperature: BBN, neutrino decoupling, $e^\pm$ annihilation.

### intermediate ($100\,000$ yr $< t < 9$ Gyr, $0.5 < z < 3400$)
**matter-dominated**. $a \propto t^{2/3}$. CMB released at $z = 1100$ marks the beginning of this era's "visible" history. structure forms gravitationally.

### late universe ($t > 9$ Gyr, $z < 0.5$)
**dark-energy-dominated**. $a \propto e^{Ht}$. accelerated expansion. structure formation slows.

## the transitions that matter most for observation

the four most-cited:
1. **BBN** ($t \sim 1$ to $20$ min): produces $\eta = n_b/n_\gamma$, the "freeze-in" of light element abundances.
2. **matter-radiation equality** ($z \sim 3400$): turn-over of the matter power spectrum, observable in galaxy surveys.
3. **recombination** ($z \sim 1100$): CMB last-scattering surface. anisotropies encode early-universe physics.
4. **reionisation** ($z \sim 6$ to $10$): first ionising sources, end of the Dark Ages. CMB optical depth $\tau \sim 0.054$.
