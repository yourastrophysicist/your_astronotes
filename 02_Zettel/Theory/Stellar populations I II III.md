Walter Baade's 1944 classification of stars by **age, metallicity, and kinematics**, originally based on resolving M31's stellar content with the wartime-blackout-darkened Mt. Wilson sky. still the foundational dichotomy of Galactic stellar astronomy.

## the three populations

### Population I

**young, metal-rich, disk-dwelling**. born from the modern ISM enriched by previous generations. examples: O and B stars, classical Cepheids, open clusters, the Sun.
- **age**: $\le 10$ Gyr, mostly $\lesssim 5$ Gyr.
- **metallicity**: $[Fe/H] \sim -0.5$ to $+0.5$, around solar.
- **kinematics**: thin disk, low velocity dispersion, near-circular orbits.
- **CMD**: well-defined main sequence with a young turnoff.

associated with **active star formation** in spiral arms and molecular clouds. galaxies dominated by Pop I light: spirals, irregulars.

### Population II

**old, metal-poor, halo and bulge dwelling**. examples: globular clusters, halo field stars, RR Lyrae, BHB stars, subdwarfs.
- **age**: $\sim 10$ to $13$ Gyr, near the age of the universe.
- **metallicity**: $[Fe/H] \sim -3$ to $-0.5$.
- **kinematics**: spheroidal halo, high velocity dispersion, eccentric orbits.
- **CMD**: prominent giant branch, horizontal branch (with RR Lyrae gap), narrow main sequence with a low-mass turnoff.

associated with the **first generation of star formation** in the Galaxy, before significant chemical enrichment.

### Population III

**hypothetical first stars**. zero metallicity, formed from primordial H + He gas at $z \sim 20$ to $30$.
- **metallicity**: $Z = 0$, never observed directly.
- **mass**: theoretical models predict massive ($> 10\,M_\odot$, possibly $\sim 100\,M_\odot$), short-lived.
- **observational signature**: strong He II $1640$ Å, no metal lines.

JWST is probing the first galaxies at $z > 10$, with possible Pop III candidates among the most metal-poor systems. no confirmed Pop III star has yet been spectroscopically observed.

## the disk vs halo

Galactic stellar populations decompose into kinematic and chemical components:
- **thin disk**: Pop I, $|z| < 0.3$ kpc, $[Fe/H] \sim -0.3$ to $+0.3$.
- **thick disk**: intermediate, $|z| \sim 0.3$ to $1$ kpc, $[Fe/H] \sim -1$ to $-0.3$.
- **bulge**: old + metal-rich, peanut-shaped.
- **halo**: Pop II, $|z| > 5$ kpc, $[Fe/H] \sim -3$ to $-0.5$, extends out to the virial radius $\sim 200$ kpc.

each component has a distinct age-metallicity-kinematic signature, traceable in Gaia + APOGEE + LAMOST.

## the chemical enrichment story

stars form, fuse light elements into heavier ones, return enriched material via winds and supernovae, and the next generation forms with higher $Z$. so:
- **Pop III**: from primordial gas, $Z = 0$.
- **Pop II**: born after first SN enrichment, $Z \sim 10^{-3}$ to $10^{-1} Z_\odot$.
- **Pop I**: born in fully enriched ISM, $Z \sim Z_\odot$.

the **mass-metallicity relation** in galaxies traces this: more massive galaxies retain more SN ejecta, so reach higher $Z$ faster. see [Chemical evolution of galaxies](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Chemical evolution of galaxies.md) and [Metallicity and chemical evolution](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Metallicity and chemical evolution.md).
