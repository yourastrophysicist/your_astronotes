# virgo cluster

up: [Astrophysics_of_Galaxies_MOC](../../00_Atlas/Astrophysics_of_Galaxies_MOC.md) · [Coma cluster](../../02_Zettel/Theory/Coma cluster.md)

## properties

The Virgo Cluster is the nearest rich galaxy cluster and forms the heart of the Local Supercluster:

- **distance**: $d \approx 16.5 \pm 0.1$ Mpc (from Cepheids, TRGB, and SBF).
- **systemic velocity**: $V_0 \approx 1050$ km/s ($z \approx 0.0036$).
- **virial radius**: $R_{\rm vir} \approx 1.7$ Mpc.
- **total mass**: $M_{\rm vir} \sim 1.2 \times 10^{14} M_\odot$.
- **central dominant galaxy**: **M87** (giant elliptical, cD-like, powerful relativistic jet, $M_{\rm BH} \approx 6.5 \times 10^9 M_\odot$).

## dynamical state

Virgo is a dynamically young, unrelaxed cluster undergoing active assembly:
- Subcluster A (centered on M87).
- Subcluster B (centered on M49, falling into the main cluster from the south).
- Multiple infalling groups (M86 group with negative radial velocity $\approx -244$ km/s).

## environmental effects on galaxies

Because Virgo is close and dense, it serves as the premier laboratory for environmental galaxy transformation:
- **ram-pressure stripping** (Gunn & Gott 1972): the hot intracluster medium (ICM, $T \sim 3 \times 10^7$ K) exerts ram pressure $P_{\rm ram} = \rho_{\rm ICM} v_{\rm rel}^2$. When $P_{\rm ram} > 2\pi G \Sigma_* \Sigma_{\rm gas}$, cold gas is stripped from the disk, creating truncated $\text{H I}$ disks, extended gas tails, and "jellyfish galaxies" (e.g., NGC 4522, NGC 4402).
- **H I deficiency**: spirals near the cluster core have up to a factor of 10 less neutral hydrogen than field spirals of the same optical diameter.
