### Master Derivations & Mathematical Rigor (Block 1)
1. **Schechter Integrals & Asymptotic Divergence**:
   $$\Phi(L) dL = \phi^* \left(\frac{L}{L^*}\right)^\alpha e^{-L/L^*} \frac{dL}{L^*} \xrightarrow{x \equiv L/L^*} \phi^* x^\alpha e^{-x} dx$$
   - Total Number Density: $n_{\rm gal} = \phi^* \int_0^\infty x^\alpha e^{-x} dx = \phi^* \Gamma(\alpha+1)$. Converges if and only if $\alpha > -1$. Since observed $\alpha \approx -1.2 \le -1$, $n_{\rm gal}$ diverges at $L \to 0$.
   - Total Luminosity Density: $j = \phi^* L^* \int_0^\infty x^{\alpha+1} e^{-x} dx = \phi^* L^* \Gamma(\alpha+2)$. Converges if and only if $\alpha > -2$. Since $\alpha \approx -1.2 > -2$, $j$ is finite and dominated by galaxies near $L^*$.
2. **Magnitude Transformation**:
   $$M - M^* = -2.5 \log_{10}(L/L^*) \implies L/L^* = 10^{-0.4(M-M^*)}$$
   $$\left|\frac{dL}{dM}\right| = 0.4 \ln(10) L \implies \Phi(M) dM = 0.4 \ln(10) \phi^* 10^{-0.4(\alpha+1)(M-M^*)} \exp\left[-10^{-0.4(M-M^*)}\right] dM$$
3. **The $1/V_{\rm max}$ Schmidt Estimator & Poisson Error**:
   $$d_{\rm max, i} = d_i \cdot 10^{0.2(m_{\rm lim} - m_i)} \implies V_{\rm max, i} = \frac{\Omega}{3} d_{\rm max, i}^3, \quad \Phi(M)\Delta M = \sum_{i=1}^{N_{\rm bin}} \frac{1}{V_{\rm max, i}}, \quad \sigma^2 = \frac{1}{(\Delta M)^2}\sum_{i=1}^{N_{\rm bin}} \frac{1}{V_{\rm max, i}^2}$$
4. **Petrosian Tolman Invariance**:
   $$\eta(r) \equiv \frac{I_{\rm obs}(r)}{\langle I_{\rm obs}(<r)\rangle} = \frac{I_{\rm emit}(r)\cdot (1+z)^{-4}}{\frac{2}{r^2}\int_0^r I_{\rm emit}(r')(1+z)^{-4} r' dr'} = \frac{I_{\rm emit}(r)}{\langle I_{\rm emit}(<r)\rangle} \implies r_P \text{ is strictly redshift-independent.}$$

### Master Derivations & Mathematical Rigor (Block 2)
1. **CAS Non-Parametric Metrics**:
   - $C = 5\log_{10}(r_{80}/r_{20})$ ($C \approx 2.7$ for exponential disk, $C \approx 5.2$ for de Vaucouleurs profile).
   - Asymmetry: $A = \frac{\sum |I_0 - I_{180}|}{2\sum |I_0|} - B_{180}$. Minimization condition: rotation center $(x_0, y_0)$ satisfies $\partial A/\partial x_0 = 0, \partial A/\partial y_0 = 0$.
   - Smoothness: $S = \frac{\sum |I_0 - I_s|}{\sum |I_0|} - B_s$ with filter scale $\sigma = 0.2 r_P$ and central core $r < \sigma$ masked.
   - Merger criterion: $A > 0.35$ and $A > S$.
2. **Madau-Dickinson Integral & Stellar Mass Accumulation**:
   $$\rho_{\rm SFR}(z) = \mathcal{K}_{\rm UV} \cdot \rho_{\rm UV, corr}(z) = \mathcal{K}_{\rm UV} \left[ 10^{0.4 A_{\rm UV}} \phi^*(z) L^*(z) \Gamma(\alpha(z)+2) \right]$$
   $$\rho_*(z) = (1 - R) \int_z^\infty \frac{\rho_{\rm SFR}(z')}{(1+z') H(z')} dz' \quad (R \approx 0.41 \text{ for Chabrier IMF})$$

### Master Derivations & Mathematical Rigor (Block 3)
1. **Sérsic Total Luminosity Integration**:
   $$L = \int_0^\infty 2\pi R \, I(R) \, dR = 2\pi I_e e^{b_n} \int_0^\infty R \exp\left[ -b_n \left(\frac{R}{R_e}\right)^{1/n} \right] dR$$
   Substitute $x \equiv b_n (R/R_e)^{1/n} \implies R = R_e b_n^{-n} x^n \implies dR = n R_e b_n^{-n} x^{n-1} dx$:
   $$R \, dR = n R_e^2 b_n^{-2n} x^{2n-1} dx \implies L = 2\pi n e^{b_n} R_e^2 I_e b_n^{-2n} \int_0^\infty x^{2n-1} e^{-x} dx = \boxed{\frac{2\pi n \, e^{b_n}}{b_n^{2n}} I_e R_e^2 \, \Gamma(2n)}$$
2. **Half-Light Condition for $b_n$**:
   $$L(<R_e) = \frac{1}{2} L \iff \gamma(2n, b_n) = \frac{1}{2} \Gamma(2n) \implies b_n \approx 2n - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{0.0079}{n}$$
   - $n = 1$ (Exponential disk): $b_1 \approx 1.6783$, $L = 2\pi I_0 h_R^2$ with $R_e = 1.678 h_R$.
   - $n = 4$ (de Vaucouleurs): $b_4 \approx 7.6692$, $L \approx 7.215 \pi R_e^2 I_e$.

### Master Derivations & Mathematical Rigor (Block 4)
1. **Spectral PCA Covariance Matrix & Eigenvalues**:
   $$\Delta X_{ij} = \tilde{f}_{ij} - \bar{f}_j, \quad C = \frac{1}{N} \Delta X^T \Delta X \implies C \mathbf{e}_k = \lambda_k \mathbf{e}_k$$
   $$\mathbf{f}_i \approx \bar{\mathbf{f}} + \sum_{k=1}^K a_{ik} \mathbf{e}_k, \quad a_{ik} = (\tilde{\mathbf{f}}_i - \bar{\mathbf{f}}) \cdot \mathbf{e}_k$$
2. **The 2dFGRS Spectral Type Parameter $\eta$**:
   $$\eta \equiv a_1 \cos\theta + a_2 \sin\theta \quad (\theta \approx -20^\circ)$$
   Directly correlates with the stellar birthrate parameter $b \equiv \mathrm{SFR}/\langle\mathrm{SFR}\rangle$, ordering galaxies objectively from passive absorption-line ellipticals to extreme starbursts.

### Master Derivations & Mathematical Rigor (Block 11)
1. **The $[\alpha/\text{Fe}]$ Cosmic Chemical Clock**:
   $$\frac{dM_\alpha}{dt} = -Z_\alpha \psi(t) + y_\alpha^{\rm CCSN}(1 - R)\psi(t)$$
   $$\frac{dM_{\rm Fe}}{dt} = -Z_{\rm Fe} \psi(t) + y_{\rm Fe}^{\rm CCSN}(1 - R)\psi(t) + \int_0^t \psi(t - \tau) y_{\rm Fe}^{\rm Ia} \Psi(\tau) d\tau$$
   - For rapid starbursts ($t < \tau_{\rm Ia} \sim 1$ Gyr), the SN Ia integral vanishes: $[\alpha/\text{Fe}]_{\rm plateau} \approx +0.35 \text{ to } +0.45$ dex.
   - For extended star formation ($t > 1$ Gyr), SN Ia iron drops $[\alpha/\text{Fe}]$, creating the "knee". In massive ellipticals, $[\alpha/\text{Fe}] \approx +0.25$ dex proves star formation ceased within $\Delta t < 0.8$ Gyr (**downsizing**).
2. **Lick Indices Age-Metallicity Degeneracy**:
   - $H\beta$ equivalent width traces main sequence turnoff temperature (sensitive to Age).
   - $\mathrm{Mg}\,b$, $\mathrm{Fe}\,5270$, $\mathrm{Fe}\,5335$ trace atmospheric metal line blanketing (sensitive to Metallicity).
   - Combined index $[\mathrm{MgFe}]' \equiv \sqrt{\mathrm{Mg}\,b \cdot (0.72 \mathrm{Fe}5270 + 0.28 \mathrm{Fe}5335)}$ is insensitive to $[\alpha/\text{Fe}]$, cleanly separating age from metallicity on model grids.

## Block 12  -  Scaling Relations & Galaxy Structure

Empirical relations revealing virial equilibrium, homology, and star formation efficiency.

- [Low surface brightness galaxies](../03_Zettel/Theory/Low%20surface%20brightness%20galaxies.html)  -  $\mu_0(B) > 23$ mag/arcsec$^2$, Ultra-Diffuse Galaxies (UDGs), dark matter domination at all radii (`gal_srs-41..47`; `dispense_DM_2_eng.pdf` p. 48).

### Master Derivations & Mathematical Rigor (Block 12)
1. **Tully-Fisher Derivation for Spiral Disks**:
   $$v_c^2 \approx \frac{G M}{R_c}, \quad L = 2\pi I_0 h_R^2 \implies h_R \propto (L/I_0)^{1/2}, \quad M = \Upsilon L$$
   $$v_c^2 \propto \frac{G \Upsilon L}{(L/I_0)^{1/2}} = G \Upsilon I_0^{1/2} L^{1/2} \implies v_c^4 \propto G^2 \Upsilon^2 I_0 L \implies \boxed{L = \left(\frac{C_R^2}{2\pi G^2 \Upsilon^2 I_0}\right) v_c^4 \propto v_c^4}$$
   Requires constant central surface brightness $I_0$ (Freeman's law) and constant mass-to-light ratio $\Upsilon$.
2. **Virial Fundamental Plane & Tilt for Ellipticals**:
   Scalar Virial Theorem: $2K + U = 0 \implies 3 M \sigma_0^2 = c_2 \frac{G M^2}{R_e} \implies M = c_1 \frac{R_e \sigma_0^2}{G}$.
   Relate mass to luminosity $M = \Upsilon L$ and mean surface brightness $L = 2\pi R_e^2 \langle I\rangle_e$:
   $$\Upsilon (2\pi R_e^2 \langle I\rangle_e) = c_1 \frac{R_e \sigma_0^2}{G} \implies \boxed{R_e = \left(\frac{c_1}{2\pi G}\right) \sigma_0^2 \, \langle I\rangle_e^{-1} \, \Upsilon^{-1}}$$
   $$\log R_e = 2\log\sigma_0 - \log\langle I\rangle_e - \log\Upsilon + \text{const}$$
   - **The Tilt**: Empirical fit gives $\log R_e = 1.24\log\sigma_0 - 0.82\log\langle I\rangle_e + \text{const}$.
   - The deviation from exponents $(2, -1)$ is caused by $\Upsilon \equiv M/L \propto M^{0.2}$, structural non-homology (Sérsic $n$ varying with mass), and increasing dark matter fraction.
3. **Faber-Jackson Relation as a Projection**:
   If mean surface brightness $\langle I\rangle_e \approx \text{const}$, the Fundamental Plane collapses to the Faber-Jackson relation $L \propto \sigma_0^4$. The scatter in Faber-Jackson ($\sim 0.4$ dex) is much larger than FP ($\sim 0.05$ dex) because variations in $\langle I\rangle_e$ are ignored.
