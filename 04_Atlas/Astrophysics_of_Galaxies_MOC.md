## Block 1 — The Galaxy Luminosity Function & Observational Basics

Statistical distribution of galaxy luminosities, selection effects, and SDSS foundations. (Slides `gal_lf1-01..50`, `gal_lf2-01..26`, Blanton 2003 figures).

- [Luminosity function definition](../03_Zettel/Theory/Luminosity%20function%20definition.html) — differential $\Phi(L) dL$ and cumulative luminosity functions, volume effects, $1/V_{\max}$ Schmidt method, Malmquist bias (`gal_lf1-01..05`).
- [Schechter function](../03_Zettel/Theory/Schechter%20function.html) — Paul Schechter (1976) parametric form: $\Phi(L) dL = \frac{\Phi^*}{L^*} \left(\frac{L}{L^*}\right)^\alpha e^{-L/L^*} dL$, physical interpretation of $\Phi^*, L^*, \alpha$ (`gal_lf1-06..10`, `blanton2003_LF_rband.png`).
- [Schechter function in magnitudes](../03_Zettel/Theory/Schechter%20function%20in%20magnitudes.html) — transformation to absolute magnitudes: $\Phi(M) dM = 0.4\ln(10) \Phi^* 10^{0.4(\alpha+1)(M^*-M)} \exp[-10^{0.4(M^*-M)}] dM$, faint-end slope $0.4(\alpha+1)$ (`gal_lf2-01..04`, `blanton2003_LF_ugiz.png`).
- [Integrals of the Schechter function](../03_Zettel/Theory/Integrals%20of%20the%20Schechter%20function.html) — number density $n_{\rm tot} = \Phi^* \Gamma(\alpha+1)$ (converges for $\alpha > -1$), luminosity density $j_{\rm tot} = \Phi^* L^* \Gamma(\alpha+2)$ (converges for $\alpha > -2$), incomplete gamma function integrals (`gal_lf1-11..15`).
- [Double power-law modified Schechter](../03_Zettel/Theory/Double%20power-law%20modified%20Schechter.html) — deviations at bright/faint ends, high-$z$ quasar and galaxy LFs (`gal_lf1-41..50`).
- [Petrosian radius](../03_Zettel/Theory/Petrosian%20radius.html) — Petrosian ratio $\eta(r) = I(r)/\langle I\rangle_{<r}$, SDSS Petrosian radius $r_P$ where $\eta(r_P) = 0.2$, cosmological $(1+z)^4$ surface brightness independence (`gal_lf2-05..08`).
- [SDSS overview](../03_Zettel/Theory/SDSS%20overview.html) — Sloan Digital Sky Survey 2.5m telescope, five-band $ugriz$ filter system, spectroscopic fiber plug plates, K-corrections (`gal_lf2-09..12`, `blanton2003_kcorrection.png`).

## Block 3 — Morphological and Photometric Classification

## Block 6 — Galaxy Kinematics & Dynamics

Stellar and gas motions, velocity distributions, and rotation curves. (Slides `gal_dm-01..22`, Cappellari 2002 figures).

- [LOSVD](../03_Zettel/Theory/LOSVD.html) — Line-of-Sight Velocity Distribution, parametrization with Gauss-Hermite series: $f(v) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} e^{-\frac{1}{2}y^2} [1 + h_3 H_3(y) + h_4 H_4(y)]$, physical meaning of $h_3$ (skewness) and $h_4$ (kurtosis) (`cappellari2002_kinematics.png`, `cappellari2002_kinematics_fig2.png`).
- [Stellar kinematics measurements](../03_Zettel/Theory/Stellar%20kinematics%20measurements.html) — Penalized Pixel-Fitting (pPXF) method, extracting $V, \sigma, h_3, h_4$ from absorption lines.
- [Ionized gas kinematics](../03_Zettel/Theory/Ionized%20gas%20kinematics.html) — emission line tracers ($H\alpha$, [OIII]), thin disk rotation, non-circular motions.
- [Rotation curves](../03_Zettel/Theory/Rotation%20curves.html) — flat rotation curves in spirals ($V(R) \approx \text{const}$ out to several $R_{25}$), mass deconstruction: $V_{\rm circ}^2(R) = V_{\rm disk}^2 + V_{\rm bulge}^2 + V_{\rm gas}^2 + V_{\rm halo}^2$ (`gal_dm-01..22`).

## Block 8 — Supermassive Black Holes (SMBHs) in Galaxies

Central engines, dynamic mass determinations, scaling relations, and co-evolution. (Slides `gal_bh-01..74`, Gillessen 2009, Miyoshi 1995, Peterson 2004, Bentz 2009/2010, Ferrarese 2000, Kormendy & Ho 2013, McConnell & Ma 2013, Saglia 2016, EHT 2019).

- [Galactic Center Sgr A and S-stars](../03_Zettel/Theory/interf/Galactic%20Center%20Sgr%20A%20and%20S-stars.html) — S2 star orbital monitoring over full 16-year period, pericenter $r_{\rm peri} \approx 120$ AU ($1400 R_s$), orbital velocity $v \approx 7700$ km/s, proving point mass $M_\bullet = (4.15 \pm 0.13) \times 10^6 M_\odot$ (Gillessen et al. 2009; Nobel Prize 2020) (`gal_bh-01..15`, `gillessen2009_S2_orbit.png`, `gillessen2009_stellar_orbits.png`, `gillessen2009_mass_enclosed.png`).
- [Water maser BH masses](../03_Zettel/Theory/Water%20maser%20BH%20masses.html) — VLBA observations of 22 GHz $H_2O$ masers in NGC 4258 (Miyoshi et al. 1995), thin Keplerian disk ($0.13-0.26$ pc), rotation curve $v(r) \propto r^{-1/2}$, gold-standard mass $M_\bullet = (3.9 \pm 0.1) \times 10^7 M_\odot$ (`gal_bh-16..28`, `miyoshi1995_fig1_masers.png`, `miyoshi1995_fig2_masers.png`).
- [Reverberation mapping](../03_Zettel/Theory/Reverberation%20mapping.html) — AGN Broad-Line Region (BLR) light-travel time delay $\tau = R_{\rm BLR}/c$, cross-correlation of continuum and emission line light curves, virial mass $M_\bullet = f \frac{c\tau \Delta V^2}{G}$, virial factor $f$, velocity-delay maps (`gal_bh-29..40`, `peterson2004_table.png`, `peterson2004_RL.png`, `bentz2009_RL.png`, `bentz2010_RL.png`, `bentz2010_velocitydelay.png`).
- [AGN spectroscopy](../03_Zettel/Theory/AGN%20spectroscopy.html) — Unified Model: central SMBH, accretion disk, BLR ($v \sim 1000-10000$ km/s), dusty obscuring torus, Narrow-Line Region (NLR, $v \sim 500$ km/s), relativistic jets (`gal_bh-41..45`).
- [Stellar dynamics SMBH masses](../03_Zettel/Theory/Stellar%20dynamics%20SMBH%20masses.html) — Jeans dynamical modeling and Schwarzschild orbit superposition method (`gal_bh-46..53`).
- [Ionized gas SMBH masses](../03_Zettel/Theory/Ionized%20gas%20SMBH%20masses.html) — HST STIS spectroscopy of nuclear gas disks, sphere of influence $r_{\rm infl} = G M_\bullet / \sigma^2$, EHT direct shadow imaging of M87* ($M_\bullet = 6.5 \times 10^9 M_\odot$) (`gal_bh-54..58`, `eht2019_M87_image_1.png` .. `_19.png`).
- [M sigma relation](../03_Zettel/Theory/M%20sigma%20relation.html) — tight correlation between SMBH mass and stellar velocity dispersion: $\log(M_\bullet/M_\odot) = \alpha + \beta\log(\sigma/200\text{ km/s})$, slope $\beta \approx 4.4$ (Ferrarese & Merritt 2000, Gebhardt et al. 2000, Kormendy & Ho 2013) (`gal_bh-59..68`, `ferrarese2000_Msigma.png`, `kormendy2013_Msigma.png`, `mcconnell2013_Msigma.png`, `saglia2016_Msigma.png`).
- [Magorrian relation](../03_Zettel/Theory/Magorrian%20relation.html) — linear scaling between black hole mass and host bulge mass: $M_\bullet \approx 0.001 - 0.002 M_{\rm bulge}$ (Magorrian et al. 1998, Kormendy & Ho 2013), self-regulated growth via AGN feedback energy/momentum driven outflows (`gal_bh-69..74`, `kormendy2013_ML.png`, `mcconnell2013_ML.png`).

## Block 9 — Interstellar Medium of Galaxies

Gas reservoirs, star formation laws, and phase transitions. (Slides `gal_ism-01..31`, Kennicutt 1998 figures).

- [Schmidt-Kennicutt law](../03_Zettel/Theory/Schmidt-Kennicutt%20law.html) — empirical star formation law: $\Sigma_{\rm SFR} = (2.5 \pm 0.7) \times 10^{-4} \left(\frac{\Sigma_{\rm gas}}{1 M_\odot\text{ pc}^{-2}}\right)^{1.4 \pm 0.15} M_\odot\text{ yr}^{-1}\text{ kpc}^{-2}$, depletion timescale $\tau_{\rm dep} = \Sigma_{\rm gas}/\Sigma_{\rm SFR} \approx 1-2$ Gyr (`gal_ism-01..12`, `kennicutt1998_SFR_Halpha.png`, `kennicutt1998_SFR_density.png`).
- [Molecular clouds](../03_Zettel/Theory/Molecular%20clouds.html) — Giant Molecular Clouds (GMCs), $H_2$ tracer via CO($J=1\to 0$) 2.6 mm line, CO-to-$H_2$ conversion factor $X_{\rm CO} \approx 2 \times 10^{20}\text{ cm}^{-2}(\text{K km/s})^{-1}$ (`gal_ism-13..18`).
- [H I regions](../03_Zettel/Theory/H%20I%20regions.html) — 21 cm hyperfine spin-flip line, optical depth, neutral hydrogen mass $M_{\rm HI} = 2.36 \times 10^5 d_{\rm Mpc}^2 \int S_\nu dv$ (`gal_ism-19..23`).
- [Photodissociation regions PDRs](../03_Zettel/Theory/Photodissociation%20regions%20PDRs.html) — FUV illuminated molecular cloud boundaries, [CII] 158 $\mu$m line cooling (`gal_ism-24..27`).
- [H II region spectroscopy](../03_Zettel/Theory/H%20II%20region%20spectroscopy.html) — photoionized nebulae around O/B stars, Strömgren sphere radius $r_S = \left(\frac{3 Q_0}{4\pi n_H^2 \alpha_B}\right)^{1/3}$ (`gal_ism-28..31`).

## Block 10 — Dark Matter in Galaxies & Alternative Gravity

Empirical evidence for galactic dark matter halos and tests of gravity. (Slides `gal_dm-01..80`).

- [Dark matter rotation curves](../03_Zettel/Theory/Dark%20matter%20rotation%20curves.html) — 21 cm HI and optical rotation curves staying flat out to $5-10\times$ optical radius, implied density profile $\rho_{\rm DM}(r) \propto r^{-2}$, NFW profile $\rho(r) = \frac{\rho_0}{(r/r_s)(1+r/r_s)^2}$, cusp-core problem (`gal_dm-01..22`).
- [Dark matter in elliptical galaxies](../03_Zettel/Theory/Dark%20matter%20in%20elliptical%20galaxies.html) — hydrostatic equilibrium of hot X-ray emitting gas ($T \sim 10^7$ K): $M(r) = -\frac{k_B T r}{G \mu m_p} \left(\frac{d\ln\rho}{d\ln r} + \frac{d\ln T}{d\ln r}\right)$, globular cluster and planetary nebula kinematics (`gal_dm-23..32`).
- [Dark matter in dwarf galaxies](../03_Zettel/Theory/Dark%20matter%20in%20dwarf%20galaxies.html) — dwarf spheroidals (dSphs) having extreme mass-to-light ratios ($M/L \sim 100-1000 M_\odot/L_\odot$), testing small-scale $\Lambda\text{CDM}$ challenges (`gal_dm-33..40`).
- [Bullet Cluster and dark matter mapping](../03_Zettel/Theory/Bullet%20Cluster%20and%20dark%20matter%20mapping.html) — 1E 0657-56: spatial separation between collisional X-ray plasma (Chandra) and collisionless mass peaks (gravitational lensing), direct evidence for particle dark matter over modified gravity (`gal_dm-41..50`).
- [Lensing as a cosmological probe](../03_Zettel/Theory/Lensing%20as%20a%20cosmological%20probe.html) — Einstein radius $\theta_E = \sqrt{\frac{4GM}{c^2}\frac{D_{ls}}{D_l D_s}}$, strong lensing mass reconstruction, weak lensing cosmic shear (`gal_dm-51..58`).
- [MOND](../03_Zettel/Theory/MOND.html) — Modified Newtonian Dynamics (Milgrom 1983), acceleration threshold $a_0 \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-10}\text{ m/s}^2$, deep-MOND regime $a = \sqrt{a_N a_0}$, natural derivation of Baryonic Tully-Fisher relation $M_{\rm bar} = \frac{v_{\rm flat}^4}{G a_0}$ (`gal_dm-59..70`).
- [Modified gravity alternatives](../03_Zettel/Theory/Modified%20gravity%20alternatives.html) — TeVeS, MOG, challenges with CMB acoustic peaks and cluster collision kinematics (`gal_dm-71..80`).

## Block 11 — Stellar Populations in Early-Type Galaxies

Chemical enrichment, downsizing, and formation timescales. (Slides `gal_sre-01..52`).

- [Early-type galaxy stellar populations](../03_Zettel/Theory/Early-type%20galaxy%20stellar%20populations.html) — downsizing phenomenon: most massive ellipticals formed their stars earliest and over the shortest time intervals (`gal_sre-01..20`).
- [Alpha-Fe enhancement](../03_Zettel/Theory/Alpha-Fe%20enhancement.html) — $[\alpha/\text{Fe}]$ abundance ratio as chemical clock: Type II SNe produce $\alpha$-elements on $10$ Myr timescales, Type Ia produce iron on $\sim 1$ Gyr delays; $[\alpha/\text{Fe}] \sim +0.2$ to $+0.3$ in massive ellipticals proves star formation shut down in $< 1$ Gyr (`gal_sre-21..36`).
- [Color gradients in ellipticals](../03_Zettel/Theory/Color%20gradients%20in%20ellipticals.html) — radial color gradients driven by metallicity gradients $d[Z/H]/d\log r \sim -0.2$ to $-0.4$ dex/decade rather than age gradients, supporting inside-out formation (`gal_sre-37..52`).

## Block 12 — Scaling Relations & Galaxy Structure

Empirical relations revealing virial equilibrium, homology, and star formation efficiency. (Slides `gal_srs-01..47`).

- [Faber-Jackson relation](../03_Zettel/Theory/Faber-Jackson%20relation.html) — $L \propto \sigma^4$ for elliptical galaxies (Faber & Jackson 1976), derivation from Virial theorem $M \propto R \sigma^2$, constant $M/L$, and constant mean surface brightness $I_0$ (`gal_srs-01..15`).
- [Fundamental plane of ellipticals](../03_Zettel/Theory/Fundamental%20plane%20of%20ellipticals.html) — $\log R_e = 1.24\log\sigma_0 - 0.82\log\langle I\rangle_e + \text{const}$, tilt of the Fundamental Plane driven by systematic variation of stellar $M/L$ with galaxy mass: $M/L \propto M^{0.2}$ and structural non-homology (`gal_srs-16..30`).
- [Kormendy relation](../03_Zettel/Theory/Kormendy%20relation.html) — photometric projection of Fundamental Plane: $\langle\mu\rangle_e = 3.02\log R_e + 19.72$ (Kormendy 1977) (`gal_srs-31..40`).
- [Low surface brightness galaxies](../03_Zettel/Theory/Low%20surface%20brightness%20galaxies.html) — $\mu_0(B) > 23$ mag/arcsec$^2$, Ultra-Diffuse Galaxies (UDGs), dark matter domination at all radii (`gal_srs-41..47`).

## Essential Galaxy Formulas Quick Sheet

$$\boxed{\Phi(L) dL = \frac{\Phi^*}{L^*}\left(\frac{L}{L^*}\right)^\alpha e^{-L/L^*} dL} \qquad \text{(Schechter Luminosity Function)}$$

$$\boxed{n_{\rm tot} = \Phi^*\Gamma(\alpha+1), \quad j_{\rm tot} = \Phi^* L^*\Gamma(\alpha+2)} \qquad \text{(Total Number & Luminosity Density)}$$

$$\boxed{\eta(r) = \frac{I(r)}{\langle I\rangle_{<r}}, \quad \eta(r_P) = 0.2} \qquad \text{(Petrosian Aperture)}$$

$$\boxed{I(R) = I_e \exp\left\{-b_n\left[\left(\frac{R}{R_e}\right)^{1/n} - 1\right]\right\}} \qquad \text{(Sersic Surface Brightness Law)}$$

$$\boxed{C = 5\log_{10}\left(\frac{r_{80}}{r_{20}}\right), \quad A = \frac{\sum \lvert I_0 - I_{180}\rvert}{2\sum \lvert I_0\rvert} - B_{180}, \quad S = \frac{\sum \lvert I_0 - I_s\rvert}{\sum \lvert I_0\rvert} - B_s} \qquad \text{(CAS Parameters)}$$

$$\boxed{f(v) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} e^{-\frac{1}{2}y^2}\left[1 + h_3 H_3(y) + h_4 H_4(y)\right]} \qquad \text{(Gauss-Hermite LOSVD)}$$

$$\boxed{M(R) = \frac{V^2 R}{G} \propto R, \quad \rho(R) = \frac{V^2}{4\pi G R^2} \propto R^{-2}} \qquad \text{(Flat Rotation Curve Dark Matter Halo)}$$

$$\boxed{r_{\rm infl} = \frac{G M_\bullet}{\sigma^2}} \qquad \text{(Black Hole Sphere of Influence)}$$

$$\boxed{M_\bullet = f\frac{c\tau \Delta V^2}{G}} \qquad \text{(Reverberation Mapping Virial Mass)}$$

$$\boxed{M_\bullet \propto \sigma^{4.4}, \quad M_\bullet \approx 10^{-3} M_{\rm bulge}} \qquad \text{(M-\sigma & Magorrian Relations)}$$

$$\boxed{\Sigma_{\rm SFR} \propto \Sigma_{\rm gas}^{1.4}} \qquad \text{(Kennicutt-Schmidt Star Formation Law)}$$

$$\boxed{\log R_e = 1.24\log\sigma_0 - 0.82\log\langle I\rangle_e + \text{const}} \qquad \text{(Fundamental Plane of Ellipticals)}$$

$$\boxed{a = \sqrt{a_N a_0} \implies v_{\rm flat} = (G M a_0)^{1/4}} \qquad \text{(Deep-MOND Tully-Fisher Law)}$$
