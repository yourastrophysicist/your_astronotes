## master year 1 — autumn 2026 exam sprint & core subjects

tracking completed modules and active Sprint 2 oral exam preparation detailed in **Exams autumn 2026 sprint**.

### active sprint 2 oral exams (15 – 18 September 2026)
- **[Observational_Astrophysics_MOC](./Observational_Astrophysics_MOC.html)** — Prof. Paolo Cassata (Oral: 15 Sep 2026, Aula A). Telescopes, detectors, CCDs, photometry, and instrumentation.
- **[Fundamentals_Astrophysics_Cosmology_MOC](./Fundamentals_Astrophysics_Cosmology_MOC.html)** — Prof. Nicola Bartolo & Prof. Giulia Rodighiero (Oral: 18 Sep 2026, Aula A). Stellar structure, FLRW cosmology, thermal history, and BBN.

### completed year 1 modules
- **[Astronomical_Interferometry_MOC](./Astronomical_Interferometry_MOC.html)** — Passed 31 Aug 2026 (Grade: 30/30). Van Cittert-Zernike theorem, uv plane synthesis, ALMA, VLTI, and EHT.
- **[General_Relativity_MOC](./General_Relativity_MOC.html)** — Passed 02 Sep 2026 (Grade: 18/30). Differential geometry, Einstein field equations, Schwarzschild metric, geodesics, and gravitational waves.
- **[Mathematical_Numerical_Methods_MOC](./Mathematical_Numerical_Methods_MOC.html)** — Written completed 01 Sep 2026 (awaiting grade). ODE integrators, root finding, linear systems, and Fourier transforms.
- **[Lab_High-Energy_MOC](./Lab_High-Energy_MOC.html)** — Passed (Grade: 28). X-ray / gamma-ray detectors, Cherenkov telescopes, Rossi XTE, and Fermi.
- **[Stellar_Astrophysics_MOC](./Stellar_Astrophysics_MOC.html)** — Passed (Grade: 28). Equations of stellar structure, nuclear reaction chains, opacity, and stellar endpoints.

### deferred modules (winter session: Jan/Feb 2027)
- **[Astronomical_Spectroscopy_MOC](./Astronomical_Spectroscopy_MOC.html)** — Deferred to Winter Session (Jan/Feb 2027). Line formation, radiative transfer, and stellar atmospheres.
- **[Astrophysics_of_Galaxies_MOC](./Astrophysics_of_Galaxies_MOC.html)** — Deferred to Winter Session (Jan/Feb 2027). Galaxy kinematics, dark matter halos, scaling relations, and galaxy evolution.
- **[Observational_Cosmology_MOC](./Observational_Cosmology_MOC.html)** — Deferred to Winter Session (Jan/Feb 2027). Cosmological distances, standard model, CMB, galaxy populations, and Pablo Pérez-González high-z lectures.

## conceptual zettelkasten directories

atomic notes synthesized from all courses are clustered under:
- **`03_Zettel/Theory/`** — theoretical derivations, physical laws, astrophysics mechanisms, and analytical models.
- **`03_Zettel/Computational/`** — algorithms, container architecture, Git DAGs, packaging, MCMC sampling, and parallel HPC.
- **`03_Zettel/Activities/`** — laboratory reduction exercises, calibration runs, and observational protocols.
- **`03_Zettel/Observations/`** — observational datasets, instrument specs, and telescope facilities.
