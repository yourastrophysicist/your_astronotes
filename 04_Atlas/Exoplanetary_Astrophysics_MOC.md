## Course Curriculum & Thematic Pillars

Exoplanetary Astrophysics covers the detection, physical characterization, orbital dynamics, internal structure, atmospheric spectroscopy, and formation mechanisms of extrasolar planetary systems.

## Essential Mathematical Equations Reference Sheet

### 1. Radial Velocity Semi-Amplitude
$$K = \left( \frac{2\pi G}{P} \right)^{1/3} \frac{M_p \sin i}{(M_\star + M_p)^{2/3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - e^2}} \approx 28.4\text{ m s}^{-1} \left( \frac{M_p \sin i}{M_J} \right) \left( \frac{M_\star}{M_\odot} \right)^{-2/3} \left( \frac{P}{1\text{ yr}} \right)^{-1/3}$$

### 2. Transit Geometry & Durations
$$\delta = \left( \frac{R_p}{R_\star} \right)^2, \quad b = \frac{a \cos i}{R_\star} \left( \frac{1 - e^2}{1 + e \sin \omega} \right), \quad \mathcal{P}_{\text{tr}} \approx \frac{R_\star}{a}$$

$$T_{\text{tot}} \approx \frac{P R_\star}{\pi a} \sqrt{(1 + k)^2 - b^2}, \quad \tau_{\text{ingress}} \approx \frac{2 P R_p}{\pi a \sqrt{1 - b^2}}$$

### 3. Mean Stellar Density from Transit Observables
$$\bar{\rho}_\star \equiv \frac{3 M_\star}{4\pi R_\star^3} = \frac{3\pi}{G P^2} \left( \frac{a}{R_\star} \right)^3$$

### 4. Astrometric Signature
$$\alpha = \frac{M_p}{M_\star} \frac{a}{d} \approx 3.0\ \mu\text{as} \left( \frac{M_p}{M_\oplus} \right) \left( \frac{a}{1\text{ AU}} \right) \left( \frac{M_\star}{M_\odot} \right)^{-1} \left( \frac{d}{10\text{ pc}} \right)^{-1}$$

### 5. Gravitational Microlensing
$$\theta_E = \sqrt{\frac{4GM_L}{c^2} \frac{D_{LS}}{D_L D_S}}, \quad A(u) = \frac{u^2 + 2}{u \sqrt{u^2 + 4}}, \quad u(t) = \sqrt{u_0^2 + \left( \frac{t - t_0}{t_E} \right)^2}$$

### 6. Atmospheric Transmission Scale Height
$$H = \frac{k_B T_{\text{eq}}}{\mu g_p}, \quad \Delta \delta(\lambda) \approx \frac{2 R_p (N H)}{R_\star^2}$$

### 7. TTV Super-Period Near Mean Motion Resonance
$$P_{\text{TTV}} = \frac{1}{\left\lvert \frac{j}{P_2} - \frac{j - 1}{P_1} \right\rvert} = \frac{P_1}{j \lvert\Delta\rvert}, \quad \Delta = \frac{P_2}{P_1} \left( \frac{j - 1}{j} \right) - 1$$

## Essential Formulas Quick Sheet (Exoplanetary Astrophysics)

- **RV Semi-amplitude**: $K = \frac{28.4\text{ m/s}}{\sqrt{1-e^2}} \left(\frac{M_p \sin i}{M_{\mathrm{Jup}}}\right) \left(\frac{M_*}{M_\odot}\right)^{-1/2} \left(\frac{a}{1\text{ AU}}\right)^{-1/2}$
- **Equilibrium Temperature**: $T_{\mathrm{eq}} = T_* \left(\frac{R_*}{2a}\right)^{1/2} (1 - A_B)^{1/4}$
- **Atmospheric Scale Height**: $H = \frac{k_B T}{\mu g}$
- **Transmission Signal Depth**: $\Delta \delta \approx \frac{2 R_p (n H)}{R_*^2}$ with $n \sim 5$
- **True 3D Obliquity**: $\cos \psi = \cos i_* \cos i + \sin i_* \sin i \cos \lambda$
- **Hill Sphere Radius**: $R_H = a \left(\frac{M_p}{3 M_*}\right)^{1/3}$
